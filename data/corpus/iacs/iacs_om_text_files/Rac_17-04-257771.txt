Advertisement for the position of a Project Associate-II

Advertisement No. RM/SCS/010 Date: 17-04-2025

Applications are invited for a Project Associate-II (one post) as a part of an Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) research proposal entitled “Studies on Inorganic Cluster based Metal-Organic Frameworks”. Interested candidates with the following qualifications are required to apply along with a softcopy of full bio-data to Prof. Raju Mondal (School of Chemical Sciences, IACS, E-mail: icrm@iacs.res.in) by 7th May 2025.

Name of the post: Project Associate-II

Number of positions: 01 (One)

Essential Qualifications:
B.Sc or M.Sc. degree with minimum average marks of 60% in chemistry from a recognized university or institute and should have qualified UGC/CSIR.

Preference will be given to the candidates with Ph.D. degree in chemistry or those who submitted the thesis and awaiting the award of PhD degree. The later candidates must produce a letter from his/her Ph.D. supervisor stating that the thesis has been submitted and the final viva/defence is pending.

Fellowship: Approx. Rs. 54000/- per month (Fixed).

Duration: The position is for three years or completion of the project, whichever is earlier.

This post is purely temporary. The initial appointment will be done for six months, which is subject to an extension of up to three years based on the candidate performance review (after each six month duration).

No TA will be admissible for attending the interview.

Application closing Date: 07/05/2025.

The applications will be shortlisted based on academic credentials. Shortlisted candidates will be requested to appear for an interview in second week of May, 2025. The exact date of the interview will be communicated to the shortlisted candidates only.

Interested candidates with the above qualification and experience are requested to apply with an updated CV (including three academicians for reference).

For further details, candidates may contact:

Prof. Raju Mondal
School of Chemical Sciences, IACS
E-mail: icrm@iacs.res.in
