Recruitment of one Research Associate (RA-I) position  

Applications are invited for recruitment of one Research Associate (RA-I) position under the supervision of Prof. Asim Bhaumik at the School of Materials Sciences, Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science, Kolkata.

Name of Post: Research Associate (RA-I)

No. of Post: One

Nationality: Indian

Duration: 12 months.

Essential Qualifications: The candidate should have a Ph.D. degree in chemistry or physics or materials science having background in optical and electrical properties of materials. Preference will be given to the candidates having experience in energy materials, Rietveld refinement, crystal structures and DFT calculations.

Age limit: As per IACS rule. Age relaxation is applicable as per IACS rule.

Emoluments: As per IACS rule.

The position is purely on temporary basis and the candidate will not have any right to claim (explicit or implicit) to any post in the Institute.

Applicants may send their applications containing complete bio-data including publications list to Prof. Asim Bhaumik, School of Materials Sciences, IACS, Jadavpur, Kolkata-700 032 by e-mail : msab@iacs.res.in latest by 16th May, 2025.

The applications will be shortlisted based on academic credential and research experience supported by publications in peer-reviewed journals. Shortlisted candidates will be requested to appear for an interview. The exact date of the interview will be communicated to the shortlisted candidates only.
