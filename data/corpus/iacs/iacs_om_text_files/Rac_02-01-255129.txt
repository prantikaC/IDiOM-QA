Recruitment of 1 (One) Full-Time Audit Consultant on contractual basis  

Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS), Kolkata desires to appoint one retired experienced official of the Indian Audit and Accounts Department in the post of full time Audit Consultant. The contract is for five years or up to attaining the age of 65 years whichever is earlier. The contract is renewable after each year subject to satisfactory performance in discharging the functions assigned to him/her by the Authority and requirement of the Institute.

The Candidate will have to deal with different audit related works/affairs of the Institution i.e., Promotion (including MACP etc), DPC cases, Recruitment matters, Implementations of recommendations of Pay Commission, Pay Fixation cases, Pension cases, Other personnel matters, Parliament questions, RTI, Public Grievance, Budget, Matters related with General Financial Rules, CAG and other external audits, Taxes, GST etc, and any other works that may be assigned to him by the Competent Authority of the Association.

A Bachelor Degree with professional qualification in Accounts/Audit/Financial Management is essential.

The Institute will offer a fixed remuneration arriving at by deducting basic pension from the pay drawn at the time of retirement, subject to maximum of ₹ 50,000/- (Rupees Fifty Thousand only) per month. No other allowance would be allowed. However, TA in case of exigencies may be paid as per prevailing norms. Eight days of paid leave of absence shall be allowed during the period of the contract applicable from the date of joining. Accumulation of leave beyond the tenure of the contract will not be allowed.

The Institute reserves the right to consider relaxation of the stipulated criteria in case of exceptional candidates, if considered necessary.

Interested candidates may apply on plain paper with their complete bio-data along with testimonials/supporting documents and email address to the undersigned at the following address latest by January 27, 2025 in a closed envelope superscripted clearly "APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF AUDIT CONSULTANT"

Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science  
2A & B, Raja S. C. Mullick Road  
Jadavpur, Kolkata - 700032.

Applications may also be sent electronically at the e-mail id: recruitment@iacs.res.in.  
Candidates shortlisted for personal interview will be informed in due course via email and web posting only.
