Research Associate-I Position in the School of Chemical Sciences, IACS

“Applications are invited for the post of one Research Associate (RA-1) in the School of Chemical Sciences under the supervision of Prof. Harapriya Rath”.

Name of the post: Research Associate (RA-1)– I

No. of post: 1(one)

Broad Research Area: σ and π-(anti)Aromatic Heteroannulenes

Essential Qualifications: (a) PhD in Synthetic Porphyrin Chemistry and/or Synthetic Organic Chemistry having good publications in peer-reviewed journals.

(b) Candidates with minimum of two years post-PhD experience in synthetic Organic Chemistry and/or porphyrin chemistry will be given highest preference.

(c) Candidates who have completed their thesis viva but waiting for original/provisional PhD Degree certificate are also encouraged to apply with all requisite documents as proof.

(b) Minimum marks of 60% in B.Sc. and M.Sc. degree are mandatory.

Fellowship: As per rules of the IACS.

Tenure: This position is temporary (one year) and can be extended further one year subject to the recommendation of the evaluation committee based on his/her performance after one year. The candidate will have no right to claim (explicit or implicit) any post in the institute.

Age limit: Should be below 35 years on the date of application.

Nationality: The applicant must be an Indian citizen.

Last date for receiving application: 6th June, 2025

Interested candidates should send their application along with updated CV along with two recommendation letters to Prof. Harapriya Rath by email (email ID: ichr@iacs.res.in.) latest by 6.06.2025.
