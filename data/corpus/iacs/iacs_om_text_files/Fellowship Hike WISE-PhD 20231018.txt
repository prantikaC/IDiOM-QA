OFFICE MEMORANDUM  

Subject: Revision of fellowship under the WISE Fellowship for Ph.D. (WISE-PhD) programme.

The fellowship for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) has been revised vide this Department’s OM No. DST/PCPM/Z-06/2022 dated 26.06.2023. The fellowship under WISE-PhD programme is also hereby revised as mentioned below:

Programme | Amount of Fellowship per month  
| Existing | Revised  
WISE Fellowship for Ph.D. (WISE-PhD) | Rs. 31,000/- | Rs. 37,000/-  

2. The revised norms become effective from 01.01.2023.

3. The WISE-PhD fellowship amount will stay the same throughout the project duration.

4. Other terms and conditions remain unchanged.

5. This issues with the approval of AS&FA, DST vide e-file no. E44615, dated 12.10.2023.
