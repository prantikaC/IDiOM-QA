Subject: Revision of fellowship under different Programmes of WISE-KIRAN Division.

The Department of Science and Technology has revised fellowships under different Programmes of the WISE-KIRAN Division as mentioned below:

Programme | Qualification | Existing Emoluments per month (in Rs.) | Revised Emoluments per month (in Rs.)
--- | --- | --- | ---
WISE-PDF, WISE-SCOPE and CSRI-PDF | Ph.D. or equivalent degree | 55,000/- + HRA | 67,000/- + HRA
WISE-IPR | M.Sc./ B. Tech./ MBBS or equivalent degree | 25,000/- (consolidated) | 30,000/- (consolidated)
 | M.Phil./ M. Tech./ M. Pharma/ M. V. Sc./ or equivalent degree | 30,000/- (consolidated) | 35,000/- (consolidated)
 | Ph.D. or equivalent | 35,000/- (consolidated) | 40,000/- (consolidated)
WOS-A and WOS-B | M.Sc./ B. Tech./ MBBS or equivalent degree | 31,000/- + HRA | 37,000/- + HRA
 | M.Phil./ M. Tech./ M. Pharma/ M. V. Sc./ or equivalent degree | 40,000/- + HRA | 48,000/- + HRA
 | Ph.D. or equivalent degree | 55,000/- + HRA | 67,000/- + HRA

2. House Rent Allowance (HRA) shall be as per Government norms.  
3. The revised emoluments shall be with effect from 01.01.2023.

5. Other terms & conditions remain unchanged.
