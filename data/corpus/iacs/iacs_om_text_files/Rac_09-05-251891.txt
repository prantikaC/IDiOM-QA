Applications are invited for recruitment of one Research Associate-I in the School of Applied and Interdisciplinary Sciences, IACS, in the group of Dr. Shinto Varghese.

Name of the post: Research Associate-I

Number of post(s): One (01)

Research Area: Organic Optoelectronics

Duration: Initially for a period of one year (12 months), the position may be extended for one year more subject to satisfactory performance after review by a committee.

Essential Qualifications: Candidates must hold a Ph.D. degree in in Physics, Chemistry, or Materials Science, with a specialization in optoelectronic devices such as field-effect transistors for charge transport, light emission, and memory applications. Preference will be given to candidates with experience in working with organic semiconductors as active materials. Strong communication skills and a demonstrated ability to write research articles are essential. Candidates who have submitted their Ph.D. thesis and are awaiting the award of the degree may also apply; however, their final selection will be subject to the rules of IACS.

Age limit and Emoluments: As per IACS rules. Age relaxation is applicable as per IACS rule.

The position is purely temporary and the candidate will not have any right to claim (explicit or implicit) to any post in the Institute. No TA will be admissible for attending the interview.

Applicants may send their applications containing complete resume including publications list to

Dr. Shinto Varghese, School of Applied and Interdisciplinary Sciences, Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science, Jadavpur, Kolkata-700 032 or by e-mail: Shinto.Varghese@iacs.res.in latest by Date 26th May 2025.

Applications will be shortlisted based on academic credentials and research experience, as evidenced by publications in peer-reviewed journals. Shortlisted candidates will be invited to appear for a walk-in interview, which will be held on 28th May 2025 at 4:00 PM.
