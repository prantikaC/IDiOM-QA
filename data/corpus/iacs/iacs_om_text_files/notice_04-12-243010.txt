Walk-in Interview for a Project Assistant (1 post) in an ICMR sponsored research project entitled “Novel cell penetrating Phosphorodiamidate Morpholino Oligomer based therapy for IBD targeting sphingosine kinase1” will be held at the School of Applied and Interdisciplinary Sciences, Room Number 147, 1st floor, Main Building, IACS, Jadavpur, Kolkata-700032 on December 13, 2024 at 2 PM. Interested candidates with the following qualifications are required to appear along with a full bio-data. The candidates should make entry their names in the interview board by 2 pm.

Essential Qualifications: A candidate should have a first class or a high second class B.Sc. & M.Sc. degree in Chemistry (Inorganic or Organic) with minimum average marks of 55% and research experience in handling the organic reaction. Preference will be given to candidates with research experience in the labeling / conjugation of DNA/RNA or polymers and knowledge on the characterization techniques by spectroscopy or any other methods.

Research Area: Synthesis of antisense oligonucleotide for biological applications on polymer support.

Age limit: 25 years. Age relaxation is applicable as per Govt. rule.

Emoluments: Rs. 20,000 including HRA per month as per the project guidelines.

Tenure of Fellowship: 1 year from the date of joining and extendable for another 1 year.

The post is purely temporary. No TA will be admissible for attending the interview.

The interested candidates may attend the walk-in-interview with a complete resume (mentioning email address) and photocopies of all certificates, reprints etc. along with the original certificates of qualification, experience etc.

Date of Walk-in Interview: December 13, 2024 at 2.00 pm in the School of Applied and Interdisciplinary Sciences, Room Number 147, Main Building, 1st Floor, IACS.
