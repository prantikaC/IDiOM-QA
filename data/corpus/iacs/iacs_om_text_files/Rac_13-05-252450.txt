2A and 2B Raja S. C. Mullick Road, Kolkata, INDIA-700032

Advertisement No:  SG/TRC/023                                                                 Date: 13.05.2025

Applications are invited from Indian Nationals for the post of Postdoctoral Research Associate in the group of Professor Suhrit Ghosh, affiliated to the Technical Research Centre (TRC), IACS, Kolkata, for conducting Translational research as per the mandate of the TRC.
Tenure: This position is temporary (initially up to 31st March, 2026 and can be extended for another 1-year subject to continuation of the project and recommendation of the research advisory committee at the end of the initial tenure. Candidates will have no right to claim (explicit or implicit) any post in the institute.

Essential qualification: PhD in Science. Candidates who have submitted PhD thesis, but yet to receive the degree are also eligible. But if selected, they will be receiving reduced fellowship (equivalent to SRF position) until the PhD degree certificate is submitted.
Desired past research experience: Past research experience in synthesis of organic compounds and/or polymers, Biophysical studies, Chemical Biology, Self-Assembly of (Macro)molecules, Photophysical studies and related fields will be preferred.
Fellowship: As per rules of IACS
Last date for receiving application: 27-May-2025 (5.00 PM)
Selection procedure: Applications will be shortlisted on the basis of past academic record, research publications, match of the past research expertise with desired requirements and other academic parameters, as decided by the selection committee. Only shortlisted candidates will be communicated via email and called for an interview for the final selection. Merely satisfying the essential qualification does not guarantee that a candidate will be shortlisted for interview. IACS holds the full right of not selecting any, in case suitable applications are not received. In case of exceptional candidates, more than one position may be filled subject to availability of vacancy. Based on the past research experience and academic background, the selection committee holds the right to recommend the placement of the selected candidates as RA-I or RA-II or RA-III as per DST guideline.
Date of Interview: Tentatively in the 1st week of June, 2025. Shortlisted candidates will be informed the schedule through email.
Method of application: A cover letter and resume with full publication list should be sent as a single PDF file through email to psusg2@iacs.res.in latest by 5.00 PM, May 27, 2025. Subject line of the email should read as: “Application for Research Associate-TRC”.
