The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS) has issued an advertisement inviting applications for the position of Research Associate-I in the School of Applied and Interdisciplinary Sciences, under Dr. Shinto Varghese, with a focus on Organic Optoelectronics. The position is temporary, initially for one year, with a possibility of extension based on performance. Candidates must possess a Ph.D. in Physics, Chemistry, or Materials Science, with relevant experience preferred. Applications must be submitted by May 26, 2025, and shortlisted candidates will be interviewed on May 28, 2025, at 4:00 PM. No travel allowance will be provided for the interview.
