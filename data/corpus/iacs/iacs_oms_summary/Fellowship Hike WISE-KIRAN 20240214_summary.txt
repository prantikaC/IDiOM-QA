The Office Memorandum issued by the Department of Science and Technology on February 14, 2024, announces a revision of fellowships under various Programmes of the WISE-KIRAN Division, effective from January 1, 2023. The revised emoluments include increases for different qualifications across several programmes, such as WISE-PDF, WISE-SCOPE, CSRI-PDF, WISE-IPR, WOS-A, and WOS-B, with specific amounts detailed for each qualification level. Grantee institutions are instructed to pay any arrears to the Principal Investigators due to this revision, while other terms and conditions remain unchanged.
