The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science is inviting applications for a Student Internship position under a SERB sponsored project at the School of Biological Sciences, with the advertisement number DS/SBS/014 dated 23rd April 2025. The internship is for a duration of two months, from 10th May 2025 to 9th August 2025, and is open to undergraduate students in their second year or post-graduate students in their pre-final year, specifically in the fields of Science or Engineering/Technology related to Chemistry, Life Sciences, or Biotechnology. The selected intern will receive a fellowship of INR 5000 per month for the two-month period. Applications must be submitted by 30th April 2025, and candidates will be shortlisted for an online interview.
