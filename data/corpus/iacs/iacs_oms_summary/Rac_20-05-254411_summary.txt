The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS) has issued an advertisement for the position of Research Associate-I in the School of Physical Sciences, inviting applications via email until June 08, 2025. The position requires candidates to have a first or high second class M.Sc. degree in Physics and relevant research experience, with a fellowship of Rs. 58,000/- plus HRA as per the Association's rules. The tenure is for two years, contingent upon an evaluation after one year, and the position is temporary with no claim to permanence. Candidates must submit their applications with a detailed CV, research statement, and letters of recommendation, and interviews will be conducted through electronic media.
