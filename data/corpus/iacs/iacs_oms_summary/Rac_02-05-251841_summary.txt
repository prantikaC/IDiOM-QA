This Office Memorandum announces the recruitment of one Research Associate (RA-I) position at the Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science, Kolkata, under the supervision of Prof. Asim Bhaumik. The position is temporary, lasting for 12 months, and requires candidates to hold a Ph.D. in chemistry, physics, or materials science, with a focus on optical and electrical properties of materials. Applications must be submitted by email to Prof. Bhaumik by May 16, 2025, and will be evaluated based on academic credentials and research experience. Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an interview, with the date to be communicated later.
