The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science is inviting applications for one Student Internship position under a SERB-sponsored project at the School of Applied & Interdisciplinary Sciences in Kolkata. The internship is part of the Scientific Social Responsibility component of the project, requiring candidates to be currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Physics or Physical Sciences. The fellowship is set at INR 6,500 per month for a project duration of three months, from May to July 2025, with an application deadline of April 26, 2025. It is emphasized that this is a temporary position with no future employment claims at IACS.
