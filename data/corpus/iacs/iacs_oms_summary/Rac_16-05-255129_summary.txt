The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science is inviting applications for a temporary Research Associate-I position in the School of Physical Sciences, focusing on the environmental effects on black hole physics through Gravitational Wave observations, with a deadline for applications set at 21 days from the advertisement date of May 16, 2025. The position requires a Ph.D. in a relevant field, with a fellowship of Rs. 58,000 plus HRA, and a tenure of two years subject to evaluation and funding availability. Candidates must be under 35 years of age, with certain relaxations applicable, and the application process includes submission via email along with a CV and letters of recommendation.
