This Office Memorandum announces the opening for one position of Project Associate-II under the Anusandhan National Research Foundation research proposal focused on Inorganic Cluster based Metal-Organic Frameworks. Interested candidates with a B.Sc or M.Sc in chemistry and a minimum of 60% marks are invited to apply by submitting their bio-data to Prof. Raju Mondal by May 7, 2025. The fellowship is approximately Rs. 54,000 per month, and the position is temporary, lasting for three years or until project completion, with an initial six-month appointment subject to performance review. Candidates will be shortlisted based on academic credentials, and interviews are expected to take place in the second week of May 2025.
