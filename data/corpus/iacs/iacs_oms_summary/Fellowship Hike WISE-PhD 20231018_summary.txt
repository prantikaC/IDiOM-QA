This Office Memorandum from the Government of India, dated October 18, 2023, announces the revision of the fellowship amount for the WISE Fellowship for Ph.D. (WISE-PhD) programme, increasing the monthly fellowship from Rs. 31,000 to Rs. 37,000, effective from January 1, 2023. The memorandum also states that the fellowship amount will remain constant throughout the project duration, while other terms and conditions will remain unchanged.
