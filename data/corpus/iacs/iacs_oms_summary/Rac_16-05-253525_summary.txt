The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science is inviting applications via email for the temporary position of Research Associate-I (RA-I) in the School of Applied and Interdisciplinary Sciences, specifically for a project focused on oligonucleotide-based therapy for genetic disorders. Candidates must possess a first class or high second-class B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Chemistry, along with a Ph.D. and relevant research experience. The age limit is set at 33 years, with relaxation as per government rules. The emoluments will follow IACS regulations, and the fellowship is for one year, with a possible extension based on performance. The interview is tentatively scheduled for the second week of June 2025, and applications must be submitted by 5 pm on June 6, 2025.
