The Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the position of Project Assistant in an ICMR sponsored research project titled 'Novel cell penetrating Phosphorodiamidate Morpholino Oligomer based therapy for IBD targeting sphingosine kinase1' at the Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science, Jadavpur, Kolkata. The interview is scheduled for December 13, 2024, at 2 PM, and candidates must have a B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Chemistry with specific qualifications and research experience. Candidates are required to bring their resumes and original certificates to the interview.
