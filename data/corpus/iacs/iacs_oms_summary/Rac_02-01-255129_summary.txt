The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS) in Kolkata is seeking to recruit one full-time Audit Consultant on a contractual basis for a period of five years or until the age of 65, whichever comes first. The position is aimed at retired officials from the Indian Audit and Accounts Department, with responsibilities including various audit-related tasks, personnel matters, and compliance with financial regulations. The selected candidate will receive a fixed monthly remuneration of up to ₹50,000, with no additional allowances, and will be entitled to eight days of paid leave. Interested applicants must submit their applications by January 27, 2025, either by post or electronically, and the Institute reserves the right to relax criteria for exceptional candidates.
