This Office Memorandum outlines the guidelines for the disbursement of fellowships and payment of fees for students at the Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science. It mandates that students receiving fellowships must adhere to leave rules and biometric attendance, with disbursement contingent on attendance verification. Fees are to be paid annually in December, with provisions for pro-rata payments for mid-year admissions and exits. All payments must be processed through the University Management System, and no grade cards will be issued without proof of fee payment. Additionally, students must clear any outstanding fees by December 31, 2022, and hostel rent must be paid monthly for those not receiving fellowships. The memorandum also states that PhD/M.Sc. degrees will not be issued without hostel clearance and that this document supersedes previous related OMs.
