The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science is inviting applications for a temporary Research Associate-I position in the School of Chemical Sciences, supervised by Prof. Harapriya Rath. The position requires a PhD in Synthetic Porphyrin Chemistry and/or Synthetic Organic Chemistry, with a preference for candidates having at least two years of post-PhD experience. Applicants must be Indian citizens under 35 years of age, with a minimum of 60% marks in B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees. The fellowship will be according to IACS rules, and the position is initially for one year, extendable based on performance. Applications must be submitted by June 6, 2025, with shortlisted candidates interviewed online on June 13, 2025.
