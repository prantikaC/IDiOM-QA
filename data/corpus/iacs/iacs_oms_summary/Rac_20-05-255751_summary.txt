The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science has issued an advertisement for the position of Research Associate (RA) in the field of Nanoelectronic and Optoelectronic Devices, with one position available. Candidates must possess a Ph.D. in Physics with a focus on Experimental Condensed Matter Physics or Materials Science, along with specific hands-on experience in various fabrication and characterization techniques. The appointment will initially last for six months, with the possibility of extension based on performance. Applicants who have submitted their Ph.D. thesis may also apply, but will only receive fellowship benefits after their degree is awarded. The age limit for applicants is set at 34 years, with applicable age relaxations. Emoluments will follow the institute's rules, and candidates are required to submit specific documents along with their applications, which must be sent via email to the designated contact by June 9, 2025.
