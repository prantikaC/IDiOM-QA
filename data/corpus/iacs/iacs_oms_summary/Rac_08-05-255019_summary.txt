The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS) has issued an advertisement for the position of Research Associate-I (RA-I) in the School of Chemical Sciences, specifically in the group of Prof. Joyram Guin. Applications are invited until May 22, 2025, and must be submitted via email. Candidates should possess a Ph.D. in Organic Chemistry with relevant research experience, and those awaiting their Ph.D. award may also apply. The initial appointment is for six months, extendable up to one year based on performance. The fellowship amount will adhere to IACS rules, and the position is temporary, with no claim to permanent employment. Candidates must provide necessary documentation during the interview.
