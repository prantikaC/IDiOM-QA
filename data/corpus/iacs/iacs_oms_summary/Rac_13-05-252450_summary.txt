The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS) has issued an advertisement inviting applications from Indian Nationals for the position of Postdoctoral Research Associate in Professor Suhrit Ghosh's group at the Technical Research Centre (TRC), Kolkata. The position is temporary, initially lasting until March 31, 2026, with a possibility of a one-year extension based on project continuation and committee recommendations. Candidates must hold a PhD in Science or have submitted their thesis, with a reduced fellowship applicable until the degree is obtained. The application deadline is May 27, 2025, and shortlisted candidates will be interviewed in the first week of June 2025. Applications must be submitted via email as a single PDF file.
