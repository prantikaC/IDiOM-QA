The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS) is inviting applications for a temporary Research Associate-I position in the School of Physical Sciences, specifically in the area of Particle Physics Phenomenology and Astro-particle Physics. The application deadline is set for 31st May 2025, and candidates must possess a relevant M.Sc. degree and either hold a Ph.D. or be due to submit their thesis within three months of the application deadline. The fellowship offered is Rs. 58,000/- plus HRA, with a tenure of two years subject to evaluation. Candidates must apply via email with a complete CV and supporting documents, and interviews will be conducted through electronic media.
