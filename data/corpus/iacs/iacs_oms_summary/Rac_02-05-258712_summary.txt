The Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science is inviting applications for the position of Research Associate (RA-I) under the supervision of Prof. Nikhil R. Jana for a DST-SERB project focused on designing colloidal nanodrugs for blood-brain barrier crossing. The position is temporary, lasting 12 months or until project completion, and requires a PhD in chemistry with relevant experience. Applications must be submitted by May 15, 2024, and shortlisted candidates will be interviewed online between May 16-19, 2025.
