This Office Memorandum announces the opening for one position of Research Associate-I/Bridge fellow at the Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science under Dr. Rajarshi Ray, focusing on system verification with machine learning components. Candidates must hold first-class degrees in relevant fields and have a Ph.D. or submitted thesis, with a preference for those not previously associated with Dr. Ray's lab. The fellowship amount will align with IACS rules, and the tenure is set for six months. Interested applicants must submit their resumes via email by May 15, 2025, with late applications not being considered.
