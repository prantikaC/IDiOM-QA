This Office Memorandum, issued on May 21, 2025, outlines modifications to the guidelines for the transfer and posting of Group III Engineers from Gr.III(1) to Gr.III(7) across all CSIR National Laboratories, Institutes, and other CSIR establishments in India. It specifies that the HR-II Division of CSIR Headquarters will now oversee the transfer process and maintain the database for rotation and transfer of engineers and technical officers, replacing the previous responsibility held by the ESD. The modifications are intended to enhance the management of engineer transfers within the organization.
