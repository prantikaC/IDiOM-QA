This Office Memorandum, issued by the Joint Secretary (Admn.) on April 24, 2025, addresses the Directors and Heads of all CSIR National Laboratories and Institutions regarding a cultural performance titled 'Jayati Jai Bharatam'. The memorandum forwards a letter from the Secretary of the Ministry of Culture, dated April 10, 2025, which discusses a mega cultural event held during the Republic Day Parade 2025, featuring around 6,000 Folk and Tribal artists. It encourages all Ministries and Departments to showcase this performance at significant national and international events to promote folk and tribal art forms and support grassroots artists.
