This Office Memorandum, issued on April 25, 2025, announces a revised composition of the Internal Committee for addressing complaints of sexual harassment at the workplace against Directors and Chief Scientists in CSIR Laboratories, Institutes, and Units. The memorandum details the nomination of Ms. Nandini Paliwal as the new Presiding Officer, replacing Tmt. S. Madumathi, effective from April 27, 2025, following the completion of the current Presiding Officer's tenure. It also lists the updated members of the committee and references previous memoranda related to the committee's formation and member nominations.
