This Office Memorandum, issued on May 13, 2025, announces the reconstitution of the Central Grievance Committee (CGC) at the CSIR Headquarters, detailing its composition and roles. The CGC will serve as an Appellate Authority for employees dissatisfied with decisions made by the Local Grievance Committee or when the Director of a CSIR Laboratory/Institute believes that the LGC's recommendations could have broader implications. The committee's tenure is set for two years from the date of this memorandum.
