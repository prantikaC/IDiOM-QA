This Office Memorandum, issued on May 16, 2025, by the Joint Secretary (Admn.), provides clarification regarding the operation of the panel for the recruitment of Technical and Support Staff under the CSIR Service Rules, 1994. It specifies that the panel of selected candidates will remain valid for one year from the date of finalization by the selection committee, as per Rule 10.3.3, and outlines the procedure for offering the post to the next candidate if the top candidate does not accept the appointment. The memorandum addresses inquiries from various CSIR National Labs and Institutes and concludes that these references have been resolved.
