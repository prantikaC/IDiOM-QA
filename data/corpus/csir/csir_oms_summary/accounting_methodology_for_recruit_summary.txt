This Office Memorandum outlines a new accounting methodology for managing financial transactions related to recruitment exams conducted by CSIR Headquarters and its laboratories, institutes, and units. It specifies that a distinct GDA Project must be created for each recruitment exam to handle all related receipts and payments, ensuring that transactions for different exams are not mixed. Additionally, it addresses how to manage excess expenditures by utilizing regular budget heads or specific budget heads for CSIR HQrs. The memorandum is issued with the approval of the Competent Authority and is directed to the Directors and Heads of all CSIR entities.
