This Office Memorandum, issued on May 9, 2025, by the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), announces the transfer and posting of several Group 'A' Chief Coordinating Officers (CCOs) based on recommendations from the Transfer Posting Committee (TPC). The transfers are approved in the public interest or at the officers' own request, with immediate effect for most, while one officer's transfer will take effect from September 1, 2025. The memorandum includes a list of officers, their current and new postings, and remarks regarding the reasons for their transfers.
