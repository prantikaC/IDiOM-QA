This Office Memorandum from the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research, issued on May 9, 2025, addresses the maintenance and repair of equipment acquired under Department of Science and Technology (DST) projects, specifically through the SUPREME scheme. It highlights the challenges faced by national laboratories in maintaining high-end research equipment and encourages all CSIR Labs and Units to utilize the funds available under this scheme for the maintenance, upgradation, and repair of Analytical Instrumentation Facilities. The memorandum outlines necessary actions, including the formation of an internal committee to identify eligible equipment, the submission of consolidated proposals for funding to DST, and the requirement to inform Headquarters about the proposals and any funds received.
