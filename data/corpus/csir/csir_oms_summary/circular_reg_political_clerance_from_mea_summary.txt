This Office Memorandum from the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research (CSIR) informs all CSIR Labs and Institutes about the new online portal launched by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) for obtaining political clearances for foreign nationals visiting CSIR facilities for various scientific and technological cooperation purposes. It outlines the requirement for CSIR Labs to apply directly to the MEA for political clearance for foreign visitors, including Overseas Citizens of India (OCI), and emphasizes the need for prior approval from CSIR Headquarters for such visits and for international events hosted by CSIR. The memorandum also provides contact information for queries related to event coordination and technical assistance.
