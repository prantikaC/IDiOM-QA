This Office Memorandum addresses the clarification regarding the induction of non-technical employees into Group-I under the revised MANAS, following representations from various CSIR Labs and Institutes. It states that the Competent Authority has determined that such induction is currently inoperative, as CSIR has decided not to fill Group-I posts, a decision previously communicated in a circular dated 21.07.2010. Consequently, any requests for induction should be handled at the respective Lab or Institute level.
