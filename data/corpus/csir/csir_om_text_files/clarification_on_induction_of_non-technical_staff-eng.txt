OFFICE MEMORANDUM

Sub: Clarification on induction of non-technical employees into Group-I under revised MANAS - reg.

The undersigned is directed to state that this office has been receiving representations from various CSIR Labs/Instt. seeking clarification as to whether the induction of non-technical employees into Group-I under revised MANAS is in operation or not.

In this regard, it is informed that the matter has been considered by Competent Authority and it is clarified that induction to Group-I is practically inoperative for the reason that CSIR has decided not to fill up the posts of Group-I, which has already been communicated vide CSIR circular letter no.5-1(88)/2010-PD dated 21.07.2010.

Accordingly, representations/requests, if any for induction of non-technical employees in Group-I under revised MANAS, may be disposed at the Lab./Instt. level.
