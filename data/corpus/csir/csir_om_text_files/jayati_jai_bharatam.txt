April 10, 2025  

Dear Colleague,  

To commemorate 75 years of the Republic, the Ministry of Culture had presented a mega cultural extravaganza featuring around 6,000 Folk and Tribal artists from across the country, during the Republic Day Parade - 2025 at Kartavya Path, New Delhi. The cultural performance titled “Jayati Jai Bharatam” received appreciation and acclaim by the audience and dignitaries present.  

2. In order to ensure continued support to the Folk and Tribal performers, Sangeet Natak Akademi has curated a smaller stage version of the same, which can feature between 50 to 300 artists as per requirement. We would request all Ministries/Departments to showcase the Jayati Jai Bharatam performance during important national/international conferences/events being organized by them directly or indirectly through their attached organizations. This will not only help promote our folk and tribal art forms, but also provide a kaleidoscopic glimpse into our rich cultural heritage, while simultaneously creating livelihood opportunities for our grassroot artists.  

3. For any information/query/tie-up regarding such events, you may please ask your Officers to send an e-mail to Ms. Amita Sarbhai, Joint Secretary, Ministry of Culture at amita.sarbhai7@gov.in so that cultural performance can be accordingly tied up.  

With regards,
