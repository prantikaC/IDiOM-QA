OFFICE MEMORANDUM  

Sub : Revised composition of Internal Committee (IC) for redressal of complaints pertaining to sexual harassment at workplace against Directors/Chief Scientists in CSIR Laboratories/Institutes/Units - reg.  

Ref : CSIR OM No. 17-02(02)/50-HR-III/CHS dated 06/12/2024  

In partial modification of the composition of the Internal Committee for redressing complaints of sexual harassment at the workplace against Directors/Chief Scientists in CSIR Laboratories/Institutes/Units notified vide CSIR OM of even number dated 06/12/2024, the Hon’ble Vice-President, CSIR, on behalf of the President, CSIR, has been pleased to nominate Ms. Nandini Paliwal, IAS (AGMUT:2002), Secretary (Higher Education/TTE), Govt. of NCT of Delhi as Presiding Officer, Internal Committee in place of Tmt. S. Madumathi, IAS, Secretary to Government, Welfare of Differently Abled Persons Department, Govt. of Tamilnadu.  

The above change is effective from 27/04/2025, following the completion of the tenure of the current Presiding Officer. Accordingly, the updated composition of the Internal Committee, with effect from 27/04/2025 is as follows:  

S.No. | Name & Designation | Role in the IC  
1. | Ms. Nandini Paliwal, IAS, Secretary (Higher Education/TTE), Govt. of NCT of Delhi | Presiding Officer  
2. | Prof. Ramancharla Pradeep Kumar, Director, CSIR-CBRI, Roorkee | Member*  
3. | Dr. Lalita Goyal, Chief Scientist, TMD, CSIR Hqrs. | Member^  
4. | Dr. (Mrs.) Manasi Mishra, HoD, Research and Knowledge Management, Centre for Social Research, New Delhi | Member*  

* Members nominated vide CSIR OM No. 17-02(02)/50-HR-III/CHS dated 06/12/2024.  
^ Member nominated Vide CSIR OM No. 17-02(02)/50-HR-III/CHS dated 07/03/2024.  

In compliance with Clause 3 of Section 4 of the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition & Redressal) Act, 2013, the Presiding Officer and members shall serve a tenure not exceeding three years from the date of their nomination.
