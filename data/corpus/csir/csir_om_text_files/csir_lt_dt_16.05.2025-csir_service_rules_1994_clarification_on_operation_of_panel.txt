From : Joint Secretary (Admn.)

To : The Directors/Heads of CSIR National Labs./Instts./Hqrs./Units

Sub : CSIR Service Rules, 1994 for recruitment of Technical and Support Staff - clarification on Operation of Panel - reg.

Sir/Madam,

I am directed to state that the references have been received regarding clarification on operation of panel under CSIR Service Rules, 1994 towards recruitment of technical and support staff. In this regard, I am directed to clarify as under:

"The panel of selected candidates prepared for the recruitment to the post of Technical and Support Staff will be alive for a period of one year as per Rule 10.3.3 of CSIR Service Rules, 1994 from the date of finalization of recommendation by the selection committee in terms of Rule 10.3.4 of ibid Rules and DoPT OM No. 14017/18/2004-Estt.(RR) dated 29.03.2004; and if the candidate who is at the top of the merit list does not report or does not accept the appointment, the next candidate can be offered the post as per the said Rule 10.3.3 in terms of DoPT OM No. 41019/18/97-Estt(B) dated 13.06.2000."

Accordingly, the references received from the various Labs/Instts in this regards stands disposed of.
