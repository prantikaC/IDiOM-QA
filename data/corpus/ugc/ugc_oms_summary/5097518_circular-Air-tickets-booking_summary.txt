This Office Memorandum, issued on 4th November 2015, circulates a clarification from the Government of India regarding the procedure for booking air tickets on Leave Travel Concession (LTC). It references a previous memorandum dated 23rd September 2015, which outlines that government employees must book air tickets directly from authorized travel agents or airlines. The memorandum emphasizes the need for all Ministries and Departments to widely disseminate these guidelines to prevent unauthorized bookings, which have been a common issue among LTC beneficiaries.
