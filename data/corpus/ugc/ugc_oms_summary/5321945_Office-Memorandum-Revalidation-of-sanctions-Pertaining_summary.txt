This Office Memorandum from the University Grants Commission, issued on April 11, 2018, addresses the revalidation of financial sanctions from the year 2017-18 that were pending payment as of March 31, 2018. It outlines procedures for various cases where payments could not be processed, including those with approved draft sanction orders, issued final sanction orders, and those pending in the Finance Division. The memorandum specifies that no fresh concurrence is needed if the proposed amounts and beneficiaries remain unchanged, and it instructs grants sanctioning bureaus and finance divisions to ensure all payments are completed before the new financial year begins.
