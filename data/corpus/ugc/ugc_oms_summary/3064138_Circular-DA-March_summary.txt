This Office Memorandum from the University Grants Commission, issued on April 7, 2022, communicates the revised rates of Dearness Allowance (DA) for Central Government employees, increasing the rate from 31% to 34% effective from January 1, 2022. It references a prior memorandum dated October 25, 2021, and outlines that the DA will be calculated based on the Basic Pay as per the 7th CPC recommendations, excluding other types of pay. The memorandum specifies that payment of arrears will not occur before the salary disbursement for March 2022 and clarifies that these orders also apply to civilian employees under the Defence Services Estimates, with separate orders for Armed Forces and Railway personnel to follow.
