This Office Memorandum, issued on 1st October 2015, announces the enhancement of Dearness Relief for UGC employees from 113% to 119%, effective from 1st July 2015. It references a prior memorandum from the Ministry of Finance dated 23rd September 2015, which details the revised rates applicable to Central Government employees. The memorandum outlines that the increased Dearness Allowance will be paid in cash and will also apply to civilian employees under the Defence Services Estimates, while separate orders will be issued for Armed Forces personnel and Railway employees. The document emphasizes the continuation of previous provisions regarding the regulation of Dearness Allowance.
