This circular from the University Grants Commission, issued on 28th November 2016, informs that Central Government Employees residing in Harsh Vihar, Delhi-110093 are now eligible for medical benefits from the CGHS Dispensary, effective from the date of the circular. Employees and pensioners of the UGC in this area are urged to apply for their CGHS plastic cards promptly, with the subscription fees for beneficiaries being deducted starting November 2016.
