This Office Memorandum from the Government of India, Ministry of Finance, announces a revision in the Dearness Allowance (DA) for Central Government employees, increasing the rate from 28% to 31% of the Basic Pay, effective from July 1, 2021. It references a previous memorandum dated July 20, 2021, and clarifies that the revised DA will not be considered as part of the pay under certain financial regulations. The memorandum also specifies that the new rates apply to civilian employees under the Defence Services Estimates, while separate orders will be issued for Armed Forces personnel and Railway employees. Additionally, it notes that the orders are applicable to the Indian Audit and Accounts Department following constitutional provisions.
