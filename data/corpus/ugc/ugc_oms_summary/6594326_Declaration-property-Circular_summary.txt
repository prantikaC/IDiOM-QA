This Office Memorandum from the University Grants Commission outlines the requirement for all UGC employees to declare their immovable and movable properties as per Regulation 18(1) of the UGC Employees (Conduct) Regulations, 1967. Employees must submit a detailed return of their assets and liabilities, including properties, shares, and debts, to the Vigilance Section by January 15, 2022, for the year ending December 2020. The information provided will be treated as confidential, and the action taken will be reported to the Central Vigilance Commission as directed.
