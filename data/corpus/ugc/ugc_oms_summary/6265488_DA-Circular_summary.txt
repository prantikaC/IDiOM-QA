This Office Memorandum from the Government of India, Ministry of Finance, announces a revision of the Dearness Allowance for Central Government employees, increasing the rate from 46% to 50% of the Basic Pay, effective from January 1, 2024. It clarifies that Basic Pay refers to the pay drawn in the prescribed Level in the Pay Matrix as per the 7th CPC recommendations, excluding other types of pay. The memorandum also states that payment of arrears will not occur before the salary disbursement date of March 2024, and it applies to civilian employees under the Defence Services Estimates, with separate orders for Armed Forces and Railway personnel. Additionally, it mentions that the orders for the Indian Audit and Accounts Department were issued in consultation with the Comptroller and Auditor General of India.
