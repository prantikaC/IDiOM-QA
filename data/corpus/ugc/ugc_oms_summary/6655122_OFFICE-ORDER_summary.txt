This Office Memorandum, issued by the University Grants Commission on April 16, 2020, outlines the implementation of the Ministry of Home Affairs' Order regarding the operational guidelines for government offices during the COVID-19 pandemic. It mandates that all Deputy Secretaries and higher officials must attend the office starting April 20, 2020, while allowing other staff to work from home with a maximum attendance of 33% as needed. Bureau Heads are instructed to create a roster plan for staff attendance and submit it by April 17, 2020. The memorandum also serves as a duty pass for officials to present when required, emphasizing the necessity of carrying their Official Identity Cards.
