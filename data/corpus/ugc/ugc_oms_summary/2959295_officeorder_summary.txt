This Office Memorandum from the University Grants Commission, issued on October 23, 2012, announces that Dr. K. Gunasekaran, Additional Secretary, will temporarily assume the responsibilities of the Secretary of the UGC in place of Dr. Niloufer A. Kazmi until a regular Secretary is appointed or further orders are issued. The memorandum has been approved by the Competent Authority and is directed to various officers and sections within the UGC, ensuring that all relevant parties are informed of this interim arrangement.
