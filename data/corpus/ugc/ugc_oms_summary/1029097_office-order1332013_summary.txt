The Office Memorandum issued on 29th May 2013 outlines the approval of various grades of consultants and their corresponding consultancy fees by the UGC Commission during its 493rd meeting on 10th May 2013. The document specifies the fee ranges for different categories of consultants, including Chief Consultant, serving professionals, retired professionals, and junior consultants, with fees ranging from Rs. 40,000 to Rs. 90,000. Additionally, it mentions that retired officials at the level of Joint Secretary and above may receive conveyance facilities to maintain parity with their previous entitlements. The Chairman of UGC is authorized to determine the consultancy fees within the specified ranges based on individual qualifications and job profiles.
