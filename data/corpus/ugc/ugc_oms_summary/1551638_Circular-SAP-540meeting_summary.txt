The Office Memorandum issued by the University Grants Commission on 14th May 2019 informs that during its 540th Meeting on 9th April 2019, it was decided to release grants to all departments selected under the UGC Scheme 'Special Assistance Programme'. It states that expenditures incurred by the departments up to 31st March 2020 under approved heads will be admissible for reimbursement, and this memorandum supersedes the previous Circular No.F.1-2/2018(SAP-II) on the same subject.
