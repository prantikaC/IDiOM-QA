This Office Memorandum issued by the Ministry of Finance on April 3, 2012, announces an increase in the Dearness Allowance for Central Government employees from 58% to 65%, effective January 1, 2012. It references a previous memorandum from October 3, 2011, and states that the provisions from an earlier order dated August 29, 2008, will continue to apply. The additional Dearness Allowance will be paid in cash to all Central Government employees, including civilian employees under the Defence Services Estimates, while separate orders will be issued for Armed Forces personnel and Railway employees. The memorandum also notes that it was issued in consultation with the Comptroller and Auditor General of India for those in the Indian Audit and Accounts Department.
