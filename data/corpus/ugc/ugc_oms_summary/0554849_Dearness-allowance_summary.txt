This Office Memorandum from the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances & Pensions announces an increase in the Dearness Relief for Central Government pensioners and family pensioners from 50% to 53% of the basic pension, effective from July 1, 2024. It references a previous memorandum dated March 13, 2024, and specifies that the new rates will apply to various categories of pensioners, including civilian, armed forces, and railway pensioners, among others. The memorandum also states that any payment involving a fraction of a rupee will be rounded up, and that arrears will not be disbursed before the pension payment date in October 2024.
