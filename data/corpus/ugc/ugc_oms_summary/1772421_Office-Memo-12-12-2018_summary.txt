This Office Memorandum, issued on 11th December 2018 by the University Grants Commission, emphasizes the urgent need for all Bureau Heads and staff to provide immediate and accurate responses to Parliament Questions during the ongoing Parliament Session. It outlines the necessity for replies to be concise, factually correct, and supported by evidence, while avoiding ambiguity. All responses must be sent via email on the same day the questions are received, and Bureau Heads are required to ensure their presence in the office, potentially working late or on holidays, and postponing any tours or leave until the session concludes.
