This Office Memorandum, issued by the University Grants Commission on 28th March 2018, circulates guidelines from the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions regarding the procedures for Departmental Promotion Committees (DPCs). It outlines a model calendar for DPCs, specifying the relevant years for which Annual Performance Appraisals (APARs) should be considered, and introduces changes to streamline the DPC process, including shifting the vacancy year to a calendar year starting from 2018 and adjusting crucial eligibility dates. The memorandum emphasizes the importance of adhering to the prescribed timelines for the DPC process and requests wide circulation of these instructions among all relevant ministries and departments.
