The Office Memorandum issued by the University Grants Commission outlines the requirement for all UGC employees to declare their immovable and movable properties as per Regulation 18(1) of the UGC Employees (Conduct) Regulation, 1967. Employees must submit a return detailing their assets and liabilities, including inherited properties, shares, cash, and debts, using the prescribed forms available on the UGC e-office portal. The deadline for submission is set for January 31, 2024, for the year ending December 2023, and the information provided will be treated as confidential. This directive is in compliance with the Central Vigilance Commission's requirements.
