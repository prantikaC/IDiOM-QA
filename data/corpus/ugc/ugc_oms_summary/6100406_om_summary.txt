This Office Memorandum from the Ministry of Finance, dated 28th September 2012, announces an increase in the Dearness Allowance for Central Government employees from 65% to 72%, effective from 1st July 2012. It references a previous memorandum from April 2012 and states that the new rates will apply to all Central Government employees, including civilian employees paid from the Defence Services Estimates, while separate orders will be issued for Armed Forces personnel and Railway employees. The memorandum also notes that it was issued in consultation with the Comptroller and Auditor General of India.
