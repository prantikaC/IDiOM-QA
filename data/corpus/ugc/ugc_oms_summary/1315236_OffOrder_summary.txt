This Office Memorandum announces the appointment of Dr. Akhilesh Gupta as Secretary of the University Grants Commission (UGC), effective from November 2, 2012, following his selection and relinquishment of his previous role as Scientist 'G' and Head of the Climate Change Programme at the Department of Science and Technology. His tenure is set for five years or until further orders, with pay fixation contingent upon the receipt of the Last Pay Certificate from the lending office.
