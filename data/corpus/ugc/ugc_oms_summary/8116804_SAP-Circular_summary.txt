The Office Memorandum issued by the University Grants Commission on 8th March 2019 informs that during its 538th Meeting on 29th January 2019, it was decided to release grants to all departments selected under the UGC Scheme 'Special Assistance Programme'. It specifies that expenditures incurred by the departments up to 31st March 2019 under approved heads will be admissible, and any reimbursement of committed liabilities will be contingent upon the expenditure being incurred within the valid period and approved allocation.
