This Office Memorandum from the Ministry of Finance, issued on March 19, 2012, outlines the implementation of a uniform date for annual increments under the Central Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2008, establishing July 1 as the date for increments. It specifies that employees who have completed six months in the revised pay structure by this date will be eligible for the increment. Additionally, it addresses a request from the Staff Side regarding employees due for increments between February and June 2006, allowing them to receive one increment on January 1, 2006, in the pre-revised scale as a one-time measure, with subsequent increments following the revised structure starting July 1, 2006. The memorandum also notes that these orders were issued in consultation with the Comptroller & Auditor General of India.
