The Office Memorandum, issued on May 22, 2013, outlines the decisions made by the Commission during its 493rd meeting on May 10, 2013, regarding the documentation of the history of the University Grants Commission (UGC) to be undertaken by Dr. (Mrs.) Kavita Sharma and the Consortium of Educational Communication (CEC). It also details the organization of various activities to celebrate the Diamond Jubilee of UGC, culminating in a National Event in Delhi on December 28, 2013. A Committee has been established to plan these activities, with specified members and a convener, and the associated expenditures will be covered by the Non-Plan Funds of UGC.
