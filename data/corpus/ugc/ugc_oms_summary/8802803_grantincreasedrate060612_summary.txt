This Office Memorandum, issued by the University Grants Commission, informs all Central Universities about the grant of an increased rate of Washing Allowance for common categories of Group ‘C’ and ‘D’ employees across various Ministries and Departments. It references a previous memorandum from September 11, 2008, which established the revised allowance rate from Rs.30 to Rs.60 per month for employees provided with uniforms, with a provision for a 25% increase in the allowance corresponding to future increases in Dearness Allowance. The effective date for these orders is set as September 1, 2008.
