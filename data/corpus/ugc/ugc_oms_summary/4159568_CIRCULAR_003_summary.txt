This Office Memorandum from the Government of India, Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances & Pensions, announces an increase in the Dearness Relief for Central Government pensioners and family pensioners from 34% to 38%, effective from July 1, 2022. It applies to various categories of pensioners including civilian, armed forces, and railway pensioners, among others. The memorandum outlines the rounding off of payments and references previous orders related to pension regulations, while also noting that separate orders will be issued for retired judges of the Supreme Court and High Courts.
