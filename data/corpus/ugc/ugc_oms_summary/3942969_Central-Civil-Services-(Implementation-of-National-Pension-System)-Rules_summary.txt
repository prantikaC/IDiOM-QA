This Office Memorandum from the University Grants Commission, issued in November 2022, announces the withdrawal of a previous circular dated 03.11.2022 concerning options under the Central Civil Services (Implementation of National Pension System) Rules, 2021, which allowed benefits under the old pension scheme for government servants covered under the National Pension System. The memorandum clarifies that this circular does not apply to employees of UGC/IUC/CU/DU & DC and instructs that any claims processed in the interim should be put on hold immediately. The issuance is approved by the Competent Authority.
