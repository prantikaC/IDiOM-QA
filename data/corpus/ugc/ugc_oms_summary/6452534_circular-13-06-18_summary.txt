This Office Memorandum, issued by the University Grants Commission on June 13, 2018, circulates information received from the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances & Pensions regarding the increase in the Rate of Dearness Allowance for employees of the Central Government and Central Autonomous Bodies, effective from January 1, 2018, raising it from 139% to 142%. The memorandum serves as a notification for future reference and includes directives for various UGC offices and sections to take necessary actions.
