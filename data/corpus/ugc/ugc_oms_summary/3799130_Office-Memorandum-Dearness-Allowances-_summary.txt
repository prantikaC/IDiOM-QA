This Office Memorandum issued by the Ministry of Finance on 25th September 2013 announces an increase in the Dearness Allowance for Central Government employees from 80% to 90%, effective from 1st July 2013. It references a previous memorandum dated 25th April 2013 and states that the provisions from an earlier order will continue to apply. The additional Dearness Allowance will be paid in cash to all Central Government employees, including civilian employees under the Defence Services Estimates, while separate orders will be issued for Armed Forces personnel and Railway employees. The memorandum also notes that it has the concurrence of the Comptroller and Auditor General of India for employees in the Indian Audit and Accounts Department.
