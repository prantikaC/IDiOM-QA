This Office Memorandum from the University Grants Commission, issued on 30th September 2013, outlines travel regulations for official tours within India, mandating that all entitled officers travel in Economy Class, with exceptions for those in the Apex scale who may travel in Business Class for international trips. It emphasizes the requirement to purchase the lowest fare air tickets from authorized sources and prohibits the use of companion free tickets. Bureau heads are instructed to ensure compliance with these guidelines and to inform invited experts in advance to prevent complications during the settlement of their travel claims.
