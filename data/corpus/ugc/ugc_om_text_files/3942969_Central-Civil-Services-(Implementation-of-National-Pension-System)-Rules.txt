NOTICE

A circular of even number dated 03.11.2022 regarding Options under Central Civil Services (Implementation of National Pension System) Rules, 2021 to avail benefits under old pension scheme on death of Government servant covered under National Pension System during service or his discharge from service on account of invalidation or disablement was uploaded on UGC website/e-office. However, the same is not applicable on UGC/IUC/CU/DU & DC employees and, therefore, the same stands withdrawn with immediate effect.

Further, if any claims have been processed in the meantime by UGC/IUC/CU/DU & DC, the same shall be put on hold with immediate effect.

This issues with the approval of Competent Authority.
