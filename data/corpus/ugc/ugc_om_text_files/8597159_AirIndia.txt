C I R C U L A R

It is informed that in pursuance of the policy of the Government of India, henceforth no claim would be entertained for travel undertaken by Private Airlines for the sectors where Air India is operating their flights. Accordingly, the Bureau Heads are requested to inform the Experts, who have been invited to attend the meetings convened by their Bureau, that they must travel only by the Air India Carrier in the entitled class to attend the UGC meetings, failing which the claims shall not be processed by the Finance Division.

All the Bureau Heads are, therefore, requested to take note of the above, and ensure strict compliance of the order.

This issues with the approval of the Competent Authority.
