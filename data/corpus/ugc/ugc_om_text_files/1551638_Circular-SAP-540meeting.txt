CIRCULAR

The Commission in its 540th Meeting held on 9th April, 2019 decided that the grant will be released to all departments selected under the UGC Scheme “Special Assistance Programme” and expenditure incurred by the department upto 31-3-2020 under the approved heads would be admissible.

Further, it is informed that the admissible expenditure incurred by the Department during the valid period and within the approved allocation will be reimbursed.

This will supersession the Circular No.F.1-2/2018(SAP-II) issued on this subject.
