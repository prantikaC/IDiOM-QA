O F F I C E  O R D E R  N O.124 /2013

The Commission in its 493rd meeting held on 10.05.2013 decided to award the jobs of documenting the history of UGC to Dr.(Mrs.) Kavita Sharma and the documentary to the Consortium of Educational Communication (CEC). The Commission also decided to organize a number of significant activities to commemorate the Diamond Jubilee of UGC with its culmination as a National Event in Delhi on 28th December, 2013. The Commission has also decided to set up a Committee, for planning the activities, as under:-

1. Prof. Seyed E. Hasnain        Chairman
2. Prof. Meenakashi Gopinath     Member
3. Dr. Indu Shahani              Member
4. Prof. A Samanta               Member
5. Prof. M.M. Ansari             Member
6. Dr. Akhilesh Gupta            Convener

The expenditure on the above will be met out of the Non-Plan Funds of UGC.

This issues with the approval of the Competent Authority.

(Archana Thakur)
