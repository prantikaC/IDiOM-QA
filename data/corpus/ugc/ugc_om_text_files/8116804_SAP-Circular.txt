CIRCULAR  

The Commission in its 538th Meeting held on 29th January, 2019 decided that the grant will be released to all departments selected under the UGC Scheme “Special Assistance Programme” and expenditure incurred by the department up to 31.03.2019 under the approved heads would be admissible only.

Further, it is informed that the committed liabilities reimbursement, if any, will be made to the department subject to the condition that the expenditure is incurred in the valid period within the approved allocation.

It may be treated most important.
