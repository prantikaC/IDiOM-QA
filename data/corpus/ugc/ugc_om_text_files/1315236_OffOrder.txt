OFFICE ORDER NO.312/2012

Consequent upon his selection as Secretary, UGC and relinquishing his charge as Scientist “G” and Head, Climate Change Programme in Department of Science and Technology, Ministry of Science & Technology, Government of India, Dr. Akhilesh Gupta has assumed the charge as Secretary, UGC with effect from 2nd November, 2012 (A.N).

His period of deputation would be for a period of five years from 02.11.2012(A.N) or until further orders, whichever is earlier. His fixation of pay will be made on receipt of LPC from the lending office i.e. Department of Science & Technology, Ministry of Science & Technology, Government of India as per terms and conditions laid down on the subject.
