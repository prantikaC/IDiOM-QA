In continuation to this Officer Order No. 82/2017, 233/2017 and 64/2018 dated 26.04.2017, 09.10.2017 and 03.04.2018 respectively, it has been decided to extend the tenure of Shri Prakash Chand, Consultant (Vigilance), UGC for a period of 6 (six) months w.e.f. 19-10-2018, as per terms and conditions laid down in Office Order No.82/2017 dated 26.04.2017.
