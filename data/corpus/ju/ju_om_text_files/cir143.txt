CIRCULAR  

It is notified for information of all concerned that, the Re-employed Staff of the University may avail Seven (7) days Casual Leave in every six-month period. The Personnel Section and concerned Department / Section / Office will maintain the leave account as stated for those staff. This is issued as per the decision taken in the meeting of the committee constituted for re-employed staff of the University dated 17.01.2025 and as approved by the Vice Chancellor dated 29.01.2025.
