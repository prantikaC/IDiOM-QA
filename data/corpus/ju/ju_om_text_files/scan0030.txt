JADAVPUR UNIVERSITY  
KOLKATA  

Date : 02.06.2025  

A walk-in interview will be held on 16.06.2025 (Monday) for the recruitment of One Junior Research Fellow (JRF) under the project titled “Development of portable electronic nose for quality assessment of spices based on essential oil detection employing molecular imprinting technology on quartz crystal microbalance sensor” sponsored by SERB, DST(File No. SUR/2022/002832) under the supervision of Prof. Runu Banerjee Roy.

Number of Post(s): One Junior Research Fellow (JRF)

Essential Qualification:

M.Tech, preferably GATE qualified from Instrumentation/Electronics/Electrical or equivalent background with interest in research. Proficiency in microcontroller/FPGA will be appreciated.

Duration: One year Four months; JRF (One year) and SRF (Four Months).

Fellowship: JRF - Rs. 38,440/- per month {Rs. 31,000 + HRA (24%)}  
SRF- Rs. 43,400/- per month {Rs. 35,000 + HRA (24%)}

Age: As per the University norms.  
Venue: Seminar Room-I, Department of Instrumentation and Electronics Engineering, Jadavpur University Salt-Lake Campus.  
Date: 16.06.2025 (Monday)  
Time: 1.30 pm  

Interested candidates may appear in person directly in the interview with their bio-data, all testimonials and completed application form (in hardcopy @ INR 50) available from the Information Section of the University in all working days. The format of the application may be downloaded from the link http://www.jaduniv.edu.in /upload files/research data/1392050161-1.pdf by paying INR 50 through the link https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/sbicollect/icollecthome.htm?corpID=627395 and the candidate must attach the transaction references details with the application form. Without all documents, candidature may be cancelled and may not be allowed to appear for the interview. Please bring the original application form and other supporting documents for physical verification at the time of the interview. No TA/DA is admissible for the interview. For any queries, may please contact at runu.banerjee@jadavpuruniversity.in / rbanerjee75@gmail.com.
