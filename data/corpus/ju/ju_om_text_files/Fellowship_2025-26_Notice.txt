Fellowship (2025-26) in Social Science and Humanities  
Jadavpur University: SYLFF Programme

http://www.jaduniv.edu.in  
http://www.iusylffprogram.org.in/iusylff

Applications are invited from suitable candidates for being considered for the award of SYLFF-JU Fellowship (The Ryoichi Sasakawa Young Leaders Fellowship Fund). This fellowship is intended to financially assist suitable young researchers of the social sciences and humanities with leadership potential. For the year 2025-26, (at least one) fellowship for MA and PhD will be awarded if a suitable candidate is found. An orientation for writing a good research proposal under the SYLFF-JU programme will be held at Vivekananda Hall, JU Main campus on 7 March 2025 from 2:30 PM. More details about the same will be uploaded later on the university website and our Facebook page. Participation in this orientation workshop is open to all students.

1. A one-year fellowship of USD 1800 for MA will be offered. MA Applicants must be enrolled as first year students in the year 2025-26 (graduating in 2026) under any department of the Faculty of Arts, School of Women’s Studies, Cognitive Science, Languages and Linguistics, International Relations and Strategic Studies, Cultural Texts and Records, Media Communication and Culture under the Faculty of Interdisciplinary Studies, Law and Management of Jadavpur University.

2. A three-year PhD fellowship (USD 7200 + USD 7800 + USD 8400, all inclusive) will also be offered and interested candidates must:  
   a. Be willing to take registration under appropriate faculty of Jadavpur University.  
   b. Have completed a Master’s degree from any faculty or interdisciplinary school of any recognized University.  
   c. Have cleared the PhD admission test as well as the interview for any department/school suitable to the proposed area of research) under any department of the Faculty of Arts, School of Women’s Studies, Cognitive Science, Languages and Linguistics, International Relations and Strategic Studies, Cultural Texts and Records, Media Communication and Culture under the Faculty of Interdisciplinary Studies, Law and Management of Jadavpur University before 15 March 2025.

Fellowship will be paid on a monthly basis in Indian rupees; the value determined by the exchange rate prevailing as on the date of payment.

The application form and recommendation sheet will be available online from 10 February 2025 and attached with this notice. These must be completed in hard copies and submitted alongside a demand draft of Rs 200/- drawn in favour of “JU-SYLFF-ADMIN A/C” payable in Kolkata. Completed hard copy application forms must be sent to Dr. Tilottama Mukherjee, SYLFF Project Director, JU-SYLFF Office, 1st floor, Dr. Biren Roy Research Laboratory, Jadavpur University, Kolkata – 700032 latest by 21 March 2025 in a sealed envelope containing all application materials. Applications may be submitted at the SYLFF office from Monday to Friday at 11 AM to 5.30 PM.
