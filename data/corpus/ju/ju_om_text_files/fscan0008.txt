The university invites applications for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) for the project entitled “The study of basic problems of stochastic resetting and its application to biological kinetic proofreading” sponsored by DST-SERB, Govt. of India under the supervision of Dr. Shrabani Mondal, Physical Chemistry Section, Department of Chemistry Jadavpur University, as per details below:

Number of Posts | JRF - One  
Educational Qualification | M. Sc. in Chemistry (Physical Chemistry Special) /Physics with at least 55% marks and NET/GATE qualified  
Desirable | Experiences in non-equilibrium statistical mechanics and Fortran/ Matlab programming.  
Age | Not more than 28 years. (Relaxable up to 5 years in case of SC/ST/OBC/Women/PH candidates)  
Fellowship | Rs. 37,000 + 24% HRA  
Tenure | Till the termination of the Scheme.

Date of Interview | Shortlisted candidate will be informed by email about the interview date.
