NOTICE  

A walk-in interview will be held on 17th June, 2025 (Tuesday) at 4.00 pm in the Department of Mechanical Engineering (1st Floor, Audio-Visual Room) for the recruitment of Junior Research Fellow (JRF) under RESPOND project “Development of Combustion Instability Model for LOX-Methane Engine”. The selected JRF will work under the supervision of Prof. Achintya Mukhopadhyay, Principal Investigator, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Jadavpur University.  

The eligibility criteria and other related terms and conditions for the JRF recruitment are as follows –  

Number of Posts: Junior Research Fellow: 1(One)  

Qualification:  

Essential: BE/ B Tech in Mechanical/Power/Chemical/Nuclear/Automobile Engineering  

OR  

ME/ M Tech in Mechanical/Power/Chemical/Nuclear/Automobile Engineering  

Candidate should be qualified in GATE  

Fellowship: Rs 37000+27% HRA per month  

Duration: Initially for two years, subject to the extension up to the tenure of the project, after assessment of the progress of work.  

Interested candidates may appear in person directly in the interview with their bio-data, all testimonials and the complete application form available from the Information Section of the University on payment of Rs.50/-. Without all documents, the candidature may be cancelled and the candidate may not be allowed to appear for the Interview.  

No T.A./ D.A. is admissible for interview.  

For any queries, interested candidates may please write to achintya.mukhopadhyay@jadavpuruniversity.in
