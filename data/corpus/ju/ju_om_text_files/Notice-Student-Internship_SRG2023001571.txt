Essential Qualification | Students pursuing Undergraduate (UG) degree in Engineering (B.E. / B.Tech. - Third or Fourth year student with specialization in Electronics and Tele-communication / Electrical / Instrumentation Engineering or similar
Desirable Qualification | Experience in IoT design and/or Machine Learning
Assistantship | Up to Rs. 5,000 per month
Duration of the Internship | Up to 2 months
Last Date to Apply | 20 Dec. 2024
Intimation | The selected candidate will be informed through email

► To Apply: Interested candidates are requested to send their detailed CV (in PDF format) and Cover Letter by email to the PI at joydeepbasu.etce@jadavpuruniversity.in. For any further inquiry, the applicant may contact the PI through above email. Please mention “Application for DST-SERB Student Internship” in your email subject line.

► Note: No TA/DA is admissible other than the assistantship.
