NOTICE  

A walk-in interview will be held for the position of Junior Research Fellow (JRF) funded by the Department of Science & Technology and Biotechnology, Government of West Bengal project titled "Regulation of multiple targets by miRNA101 in maintenance of cardiomyocyte and cardiac fibroblast homeostasis: New insight onto the mechanism of cardiovascular diseases" (Memo No: 130(Sanc.)/STBT-13015/9/2025-WBSTSC SEC), under the supervision of Dr. Arunima Sengupta, Assistant Professor and Principal Investigator, Department of Life Science and Biotechnology, Jadavpur University, as per the details below:  

Number of Posts: Junior Research Fellow (JRF): 1(One) (purely temporary);  

Qualification: Post-Graduate Degree (M.Sc./M.Tech) in Microbiology / Biotechnology / Life Sciences /Environmental Biotechnology OR any field of Biology.  

Desirable Qualification: Knowledge and Hands-on experience in molecular biology techniques, animal cell culture techniques and handling of animals. Qualified in GATE/NET/or other equivalent national level exam. NET is necessary for enrolment in the PhD program at JU.  

Duration: Two Years and Nine Months

The prescribed application form will be available from the University Information counter for Rs. 50/-. Or by downloading from https://jadavpuruniversity.in/download-centre/application-form-research-2-in-a-set/ with online payment through https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/sbicollect/icollecthome.htm?corpID=627395 , Without all documents, the candidature may be cancelled and may not be allowed to appear for the Interview. Candidates will not be entitled to claim for regular appointments/absorption in this Institute.  

No T.A./ D.A. is admissible for interview.
