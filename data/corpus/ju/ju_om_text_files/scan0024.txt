NOTICE  

A walk-in interview will be held for the position of Junior Research Fellow (JRF) in the Department of Science & Technology and Biotechnology, Government of West Bengal-funded research project titled "Exploring the fibrillations of Human Insulin, Amyloid Beta peptides, and α-Synuclein proteins adsorbed on Langmuir-Blodgett Films of Lipids and their possible mitigations from a multi-spectroscopic approach" (Memo No.:1473(Sanc.)/STBT-13015/18/2025-ST SEC), under the supervision of Prof. (Dr.) Joydeep Chowdhury, Professor and Principal Investigator, Department of Physics, Jadavpur University, as per the details below:  

Number of Posts: Junior Research Fellow (JRF): 1(One) (purely temporary);  

Qualification: M.Sc in Physics from any recognized University with at least 60% marks or equivalent CGPA  

Desirable Qualification: Knowledge and Hands-on experience in Optics and Material Physics. Qualified in GATE/NET/or other equivalent national level exam is compulsory.

The prescribed application form will be available from the University Information counter for Rs. 50/- Or by downloading from https://jadavpuruniversity.in/download-centre/application-form-research-2-in-a-set/ with online payment through https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/sbicollect/icollecthome.htm?corpID=627395 , Without all documents, the candidature may be cancelled and may not be allowed to appear for the Interview. Candidates will not be entitled to claim for regular appointments/absorption in this Institute.  

No T.A./ D.A. is admissible for interview.
