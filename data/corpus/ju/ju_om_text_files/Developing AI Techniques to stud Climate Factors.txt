NOTICE  

A walk-in interview will be held for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) under the project entitled “Developing AI Techniques to Study Varying Spatio-Temporal Relationships Between Air Pollutants and Climatic Factors”, funded by CSIR-ASPIRE. The selected JRF will work under the supervision of Prof. Sarbani Roy, Principal Investigator, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Jadavpur University and Dr. Debajit Datta, Co-Investigator, Department of Geography, Jadavpur University.  

The eligibility criteria and other related terms and conditions for the JRF recruitment are as follows -  

Position | Junior Research Fellow - 1(One) Post  

Essential Qualification  
• M.S./M.Sc./B.E./B.Tech or equivalent degree in Computer Sc. and Engg/Electronics Engg/Information Technology/Data Science/Earth Science/Geosciences or equivalent with minimum 55% marks  
• Qualified the UGC/CSIR NET and/or GATE  

Desirable Qualification  
• Knowledge of programming in C/C++, Java/Python and/or Matlab, R, Google earth engine, GIS.  

Period of appointment  
3 years (Initially for 1 year and further extendable subject to satisfactory performance or up to the termination of the project (subject to availability of funds) whichever is earlier. Position is purely temporary)  

Age  
Not more than 28 years, as on the date of interview. The upper age limit is relaxable up to 5 years in the case of candidates belonging to scheduled castes/tribes/OBC, women and physically handicapped candidates  

Fellowship  
Rs. 31000/- p.m. (as per the CSIR guideline)
