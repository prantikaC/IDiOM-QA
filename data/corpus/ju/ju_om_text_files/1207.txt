Sub: Commencement of SC/ST/OBC Scholarships 2025–26.  

Sir/Madam,  

In reference to the above mentioned subject, this is to inform you that the application process for the Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC students (2025–26) has already commenced. All Institutions are hereby requested to inform eligible students to apply online. After submission, applications are to be verified by the college authorities on the portal. Hardcopies of the verified applications must also be submitted to this office. It is important to note that starting this financial year, biometric authentication of students has been made mandatory for all OASIS scholarship applications.  

It must be ensured that no eligible student is left out from applying for these scholarships. Your cooperation in ensuring the smooth and timely implementation of these scholarship schemes is solicited.
