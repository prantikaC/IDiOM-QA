JADAVPUR UNIVERSITY  
KOLKATA  

Date: 27.02.2025  

NOTICE  

A walk-in-interview will be held for the position of a Project Assistant – under Global Jadavpur University Alumni Foundation funded research project titled “Crip Lit Cards,” of which Dr. Ishan Chakraborty, Assistant Professor, Department of English, Jadavpur University is the Principal Investigator. The project aims at developing an inclusive Card Game which would expose students to the critical tool of Disability Studies, and enable them to challenge the literary canon, which perpetuates, and is, in turn, perpetuated by ableism. The relevant details about the post are given below:  

Number of Posts: Project Assistant: 1 (one, purely temporary)  

Qualification:  
Essential Qualification:  

1. Bachelor degree in any discipline with a minimum of 55% marks  
2. Proficiency in basic computer operations (including the use of digital tools and photo editing software)  
3. Proficiency in both written and verbal communication in English  

Desirable Qualification:  

1. Knowledge of disability-related issues  

Duration: 10 months (initially)  

Remuneration: Rs. 18,000 (consolidated) per month  

Interested candidates may appear in person directly on March 25, 2025 (Tuesday) at 01:00 PM at the Department of English, First floor, UG Arts Building (near gate no. 4), Jadavpur University, Kolkata 700032, with their bio-data, all relevant testimonials and the duly filled in application form available at the University Information Counter on payment of Rs. 50/-. The form may also be downloaded from here https://jadavpuruniversity.in/download-centre/application-form-research-2-in-a-set/ and the online payment of Rs. 50/- is to be made through this link https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/sbicollect/icollecthome.htm?corpID=627395 .
