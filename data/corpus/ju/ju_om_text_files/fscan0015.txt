NOTICE  

Walk-in-interview will be held for the position of a Project Research Scientist-I (Non-Medical) position in an ICMR-funded research project titled "Surveillance of the Metal-induced Environmental Antimicrobial Resistome to Develop a Novel Indigenous MicroOrganism (IMO) based Antimicrobial Resistance Combat Strategy" (Proposal ID IIRPSG-2024-01-03057), under the supervision of Dr. Paltu Kumar Dhal, Assistant Professor and Principal Investigator, Department of Life Science and Biotechnology, Jadavpur University as per details below:  

Number of Posts: Project Research Scientist-I (Non-Medical): 1(One) (purely temporary);  

Qualification: First Class Post-Graduate Degree (M.Sc./M.Tech) in Microbiology / Biotechnology / Life Sciences and Environmental Biotechnology (with Mathematics and Chemistry as the subjects in class 12). Working experience in metagenomics, bioinformatics, microbiology, or related fields for at least two years.  

Or  

Ph.D. in Microbiology / Biotechnology / Environmental Science / Life Sciences  

Desirable Qualification: Hands-on experience in molecular biology techniques, microbial culturing, and high-throughput sequencing data analysis. Knowledge of heavy metal-microbe interactions and bioinformatics pipelines will be an added advantage. Qualified in GATE/NET/or other equivalent national level exam.  

Duration: Three Years  

Fellowship: ₹56,000 + 30% HRA (₹72,800 per month) with 3% increment in the 3rd Year (As per ICMR Guidelines). The progress of the candidate's project work will be evaluated every six months and terminated if it is unsatisfactory.  

Interested candidates may walk in with an application form and proof of their publications, working experience and mark sheets on 20th March 2025 (Thursday) from 12 PM in the Department of Life Science and Biotechnology, Jadavpur University. The prescribed application form will be available from the University Information counter for Rs. 50/-. Or by downloading from https://jadavpuruniversity.in/download-centre/application-form-research-2-in-a-set/ with online payment through https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/sbicollect/icollecthome.htm?corpID=627395 , Without all documents, the candidature may be cancelled and may not be allowed to appear for the Interview. Candidates will not be entitled to claim for regular appointments/absorption in this Institute.  

No T.A./ D.A. is admissible for interview.
