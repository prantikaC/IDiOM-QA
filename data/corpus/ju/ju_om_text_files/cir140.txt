HEALTH CAMP FOR JU EMPLOYEES OF SALT LAKE CAMPUS  

This is for information of all concerned that on the basis of the approval of the Pro Vice Chancellor and the Chairman of the Employees’ Welfare Committee, the Proposal of R.G.STONE Urology Hospital to organize a general Health Check-up and Medical “Awareness Session on Cancer” is finalized. The Chairman of the Employees’ Welfare committee has directed “Employees’ Welfare Unit” to look into the program implementation.

The awareness program is being organized by the University on 12th June2025 where oncologist Dr. Abhra Mondal, will deliver his speech and interact with JU Communities.

Program date: 12th June 2025, Thursday  
Venue: SMCC Building, Salt lake Campus  
Time: 11.30 am onwards (Registration Time-11.30am to 3.30 pm)  

Schedule:  
1. Interactive Session on awareness of Cancer.  
2. General Health check-up:  
   • BP, Weight, BMI, Random Sugar  
   • Urea, Creatinine, E.C.G(As per Doctor’s advice),Doctor’s Consultation  

All interested members of JU Communities are requested to be present there for a successful event. We look forward to have participation from all for a healthy community. Cooperation from all concerned is highly solicited.
