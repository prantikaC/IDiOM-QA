NOTICE  

Online Applications are sought for one (1) post of Project Assistant (PA) under the DVC sponsored research project titled “Cooling Tower Fog Harvesting using Wettability-Engineered Meshes”. The post is for a project funded by Damodar Valley Corporation and the post is co-terminus with the project duration. The selected PA will work under the supervision of Prof. Ranjan Ganguly, Principal Investigator of the Project, Department of Power Engineering, Jadavpur University, Salt Lake Campus. The eligibility and recruitments are as follows:  

Number of Post : Project Assistant PA–1 (One).  
Essential Qualification: BE/B. Tech in Mechanical/ Power/ Aerospace/ Chemical/ Production/ Metallurgical Engineering with 60% marks or a CGPA of 6.75 and above.  

Desirable Qualification: Preference will be given to candidates having experience in experimental work on Surface Science / Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)/ Arduino-based lab automation/ image processing in Matlab or Python  

Fellowship : Rs. 20,000/- per month consolidated.  

Age : The age limit for PA as on the day on which the application is made not more than 28 years (relaxable up to 5 years in case of SC/ST/OBC/Woman/PH Candidates).  

Tenure : Provisionally up to February 10, 2026, but may be amended according to the termination of the project and availability of fund.  

Note:  
• Interested candidates may apply in the prescribed form with their Bio-data, as well as attested copies of a Photo ID (passport/ voters’ id/ PAN card/ valid University I-Card), Academic Certificates, Mark Sheets and Testimonials. The originals are to be produced at the time of interview.  
• The prescribed form will be available from the University Website Link : https://jadunivpuruniversity.in/download-centre/application-form-research-2-in-a-set/ to be downloaded with an online payment of Rs. 50/- via https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/sbicollect/icollecthome.htm?corpID=627395  
• PDF of the completed application packet may be e-mailed to the PI at ranjan.ganguly@jadavpuruniversity.in by June 10, 2025. The email may be sent with a subject titled “Application for PA for DVC-sponsored Project: Last Name, First Name”  
• Shortlisted candidates will be informed by email. No TA/DA is admissible for attending the interview.
