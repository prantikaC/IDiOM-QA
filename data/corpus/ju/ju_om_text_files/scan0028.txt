NOTICE  

An Walk-in/ Hybrid interview will be held on 10th June, 2025 (Tuesday) at 2.30 PM for the recruitment of one post of Junior Research Fellow under WBDST Project (File No.: STBT-11012(16)/2/2023-ST SEC) entitled “Investigation on the enhanced phase change materials (PCMs) for the solar thermal energy storage devices”. The selected fellow will work under the supervision of Dr. Nirnmalendu Biswas, Principal Investigator of the Project, Department of Power Engineering, Jadavpur University, Salt Lake Campus. The eligibility and recruitments are as below:  

Number of Post : Junior Research Fellow–1 (One).  
Essential Qualification: i) Bachelors’ degree in Mechanical/ Power/ Automobile/ Aerospace/ Chemical Engg. with Masters in any of the above or related disciplines with courses in Thermal Engineering/Thermal Hydraulics/Fluid Mechanics/ Nanoscience and Technology.  
ii) Candidate should be GATE qualified in the relevant discipline.  
iii) Candidates with Bachelors’ degree in any of the disciplines mentioned in (i) with GATE qualification and fulfilling the norms for admission to PhD program according to latest UGC and University regulations may also apply.  
Desirable Qualification: Preference will be given to candidates having experience in Experimental Work/ Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD).  
Fellowship : Rs. 25,000/- (consolidated) per month.  
Age : The age limit for JRF as on the day on which the application is made not more than 30 years (relaxable up to 5 years in case of SC/ST/OBC/Woman/PH Candidates).  
Tenure : Initially up to 1st March 2027, but may be amended till the termination of the project.  

Note:  
Interested candidates may appear in person directly in the interview with their bio-data, all testimonials and completed application form (in hardcopy @ INR 50) available from the Information Section of the University in all working days. The format of the application may be downloaded from the link http://www.jaduniv.edu.in /upload files/research data/1392050161-1.pdf by paying INR 50 through the link https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/sbicollect/icollecthome.htm?corpID=627395 and the candidate must attach the transaction references details with the application form. Without all documents, candidature may be cancelled and may not be allowed to appear for the interview. Please bring the original application form and other supporting documents for physical verification at the time of the interview. The venue of the physical interview is the Meeting Room, Power Engineering Department, Jadavpur University. No TA/DA is admissible for the interview.  
For any queries, may please contact at nbiswas.pe@jadavpuruniversity.in. Out stationed candidates may also apply with completed Application Form along with proof of payment, certificates, mark sheets and testimonials as attachments within 12 PM, 20th April, 2025. Candidates will be informed by e-mail with the meeting-id (for online mode).
