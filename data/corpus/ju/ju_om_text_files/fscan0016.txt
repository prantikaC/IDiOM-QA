NOTICE  

A walk-in-interview will be held at the Seminar room (1st Floor, UG building) of the Department of Physics, Jadavpur University on 19th March, 2025 at 1 pm, for the recruitment of one (01) project fellow under the project entitled “Development of efficient electrocatalysts based hydrogen production through water splitting” funded by M/S Magellanium Technologies Pvt. Ltd., vide order No. (Ref. No. MAGTECH/PROJ/JU/015/2024; dated 3.9.24) under the supervision of Prof. Kalyan Kumar Chattopadhyay (principal investigator), Department of Physics, Jadavpur University.

Number of posts: Project fellow- one (01)  

Essential qualification: M.Sc. in Physics / M.Tech in Nanoscience and Technology; (NET / GATE qualified candidate may be preferred); at least four years of research experience in the relevant field.

Desired Qualifications: skill in performing catalyst synthesis; experience in electrochemical hydrogen production, handling of sophisticated characterization equipment etc.

Age Limit: Not more than 32 years as on the date of advertisement.

Fellowship: Rs. 42,550/- per month (consolidated)

Tenure of the Fellowship: Initially for one year; may be extended till the termination of the project upon satisfactory performance

Relaxation on Qualification and Age: SC/ST/OBC/WOMEN candidates will get relaxation as per Govt. of India rules.

Type of selection: Walk-in-interview

Venue: Seminar room, Department of Physics, 1st Floor of PG building, Jadavpur University

The prescribed application form (price Rs. 50/-) is to be obtained from the Information Section of the University on all working days. Originals along with self-attested copies of certificates, mark sheets and testimonials along with duly field application forms(s) are to be produced at the time of interview. No TA/DA is admissible for attending the interview.

For any further inquiry, interested candidates may contact Prof. Kalyan Kumar Chattopadhyay, Principal Investigator, Department of Physics, Jadavpur University (kkc.juphy@gmail.com) with the subject line “Enquiry for Mag-Tech Project fellow”.
