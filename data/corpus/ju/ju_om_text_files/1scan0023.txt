NOTICE  

A walk-in interview will be held at the seminar room (1st Floor, PG Science building) of the Department of Physics, Jadavpur University on May 23, 2025 (Friday) from 3:00 PM onwards for the recruitment of one (01) Junior Research Fellow (JRF) under the project entitled “Study of electrochemical performance of thoroughly characterized 2D MXene supported transition metal based trimetallic MM’Co2O4/Ti3C2; where M= Ni, Mn, M’ = Zn, Fe, heterostructures as supercapacitor electrode material targeting to develop a prototype supercapattery” sponsored by Anusandhan national Research Foundation-Inclusivity Research Grant (ANRF-IRG) Program, Govt. of India, New Delhi vide Sanction Order No. ANRF/IRG/2024/000340/PS-G under the supervision of Dr. Rituparna Mondal (PI), Dept. of Physics, Jadavpur University as per details below:  

Number of Posts: 01 (Junior Research Fellow)  

Essential Qualification: M.Sc. in Physics with 60% marks and above (GATE/NET is preferable).  

Desirable Qualification: Skill in performing experiments, handling Electrochemical Workstation and analysis using advanced instruments.  

Age Limit: Not more than 30 years as on the date of advertisement.  

Fellowship: As per DST-ANRF (DST-SERB) norms  

Duration: 02 years or till the completion of Project.  

Type of Selection: Walk-in-Interview  

Date and Time: 23rd May, 2025 from 3:00 PM onwards  

Venue: Seminar room of the Department of Physics, Jadavpur University located on 1st Floor of PG Science building.  

Interested candidates may appear directly with application form (Rs. 50/-), obtained from the Information section of the University on all working days. Please mention “Application for Junior Research Fellow-ANRF-IRG” on top of the application. Originals along with self-attested copies of certificates, marksheets and testimonials along with duly filled application form(s) are to be produced at the time of interview on May 23, 2025 (Friday) from 3:00 PM onwards. Without all documents, the candidature may be cancelled and may not be allowed to appear for the Interview.  

For any further inquiry, interested candidates may contact Dr. Rituparna Mondal, Principal Investigator, Department of Physics, Jadavpur University (rituparna.physics@jadavpuruniversity.in) with the subject line “Enquiry for ANRF-IRG Junior Research Fellow”  

No T.A./D.A. is admissible for interview.
