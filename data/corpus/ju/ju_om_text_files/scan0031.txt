A walk-in-Interview will be held at the Committee Room (4th Floor, Prayukti Bhaban) of the Department of Electronics and Tele-Communication Engineering, Jadavpur University, on 19th June 2025 at 11 am, for the recruitment of One (01) Junior Research fellow (JRF) under the project entitled “Real-Time Rail Track Safety Monitoring System Using Multi-Modal Data Integration of LIDAR and Thermal Imaging”, funded by Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF), Govt. of India, vide sanction order ANRF/ECRG/2024/002861/ENS, Dated 4th June 2025 under the supervision of Dr. Chinmoy Ghorai, Principal Investigator, Dept. of Electronics and Tele-Communication Engineering, Jadavpur University, as per details below:  

Number of Posts | Junior Research Fellow – One (01)  
Essential Qualification | B.E./ B.Tech and M.E./ M.Tech in Electronics & Tele-communication Engineering / Computer Science & Engineering/ Information Technology/ related field with at least 75% marks or 8 CGPA (whichever applicable)  
Desired qualification | i. GATE qualification in the specified/ allied field  
| ii. Sound knowledge in Internet of Things (IoT), Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI  
| iii. Proficiency in Python Programming  
| iv. Publications in the relevant field if any

Relaxation on qualification and age | PC/SC/ST/OBC/WOMEN candidate will get relaxation as per Govt. of India Rules

For any further inquiry, interested candidates may contact Dr. Chinmoy Ghorai, Principal Investigator, Department of Electronics and Tele-Communication Engineering, Jadavpur University, at cghorai.etce@jadavpuruniversity.in with the subject line “Enquiry for ANRF JRF Fellow”.
