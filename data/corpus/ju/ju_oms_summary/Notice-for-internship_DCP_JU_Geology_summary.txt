This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University announces the invitation for online applications for a single post of student internship under a SERB-sponsored project focused on geochemistry and isotope systematics of pyrite. The internship, supervised by Dr. Dipak C Pal, requires candidates to be post-graduate students or have a master's degree in Geology or Applied Geology, with a minimum of 60% marks. The selected intern will receive a fellowship of Rs. 5000 per month for a duration of 2 months. Interested candidates must apply via email by April 23, 2025, with specified documents, and shortlisted candidates will be contacted for an interview.
