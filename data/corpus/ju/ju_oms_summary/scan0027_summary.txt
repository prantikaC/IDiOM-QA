This Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the position of Junior Research Fellow (JRF) in a research project funded by the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) at Jadavpur University, Kolkata. The project, titled 'Electroless Nickel Coatings on 3D-Printed ABS Parts: A Sustainability Approach,' is supervised by Prof. (Dr.) Tapan Kr. Barman. The interview is scheduled for 11th June 2025 at 11:00 AM, and candidates must possess a B.E./B.Tech. in relevant engineering fields and be GATE qualified, with a preference for those holding an M.E./M.Tech. The fellowship will be in accordance with ANRF norms, and candidates are required to pay a fee of Rs. 50/- for the application form. It is emphasized that candidates must bring all necessary documents, as failure to do so will result in disqualification from the interview.
