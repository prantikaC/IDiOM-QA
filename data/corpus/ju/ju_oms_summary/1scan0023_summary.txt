This Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) at Jadavpur University, scheduled for May 23, 2025, at 3:00 PM in the seminar room of the Department of Physics. The position is part of a project funded by the Anusandhan National Research Foundation-Inclusivity Research Grant, focusing on the electrochemical performance of specific materials for supercapacitor applications. Candidates must hold an M.Sc. in Physics with at least 60% marks, and experience with electrochemical workstations is preferred. The fellowship will adhere to DST-ANRF norms, and the position is available for a duration of two years or until project completion. An application fee of Rs. 50 is required, and candidates must bring original documents and self-attested copies to the interview. No travel allowances will be provided.
