This Office Memorandum invites online applications for five student intern positions under a DVC Sponsored Research Project on Retro-fitted Electric Mobility at Jadavpur University, with internships starting from June 15, 2025, for a duration of two months. The positions include three interns in various engineering fields with a fellowship of Rs. 5000 per month, and one intern with a higher qualification and experience in 3D printing, offering Rs. 10000 per month. Interested candidates must apply via email by June 5, 2025, and shortlisted candidates will be contacted for interviews. No travel allowances are provided, and internship fees for non-JU candidates will be reimbursed upon successful completion.
