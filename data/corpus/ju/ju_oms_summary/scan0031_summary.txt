This Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) at Jadavpur University, scheduled for 19th June 2025 at 11 am. The position is part of a project funded by the Anusandhan National Research Foundation, focusing on a Real-Time Rail Track Safety Monitoring System. Candidates must possess a B.E./B.Tech and M.E./M.Tech in relevant fields with a minimum of 75% marks or 8 CGPA, and desirable qualifications include GATE qualification and proficiency in Python. The fellowship offers INR 37,000 plus HRA for the first 24 months, increasing to INR 42,000 thereafter. Applications must be submitted by 17th June 2025, with a fee of INR 50, and no travel allowances will be provided for the interview.
