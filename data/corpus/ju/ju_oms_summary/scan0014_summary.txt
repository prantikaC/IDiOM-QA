This Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) at the School of Natural Product Studies, Jadavpur University, scheduled for April 04, 2025, at 11:30 AM. The position is part of a project sponsored by the Central Council for Research in Ayurvedic Sciences, focusing on an Ayurvedic formulation for diabetes management. Candidates must possess an M.Pharm or M.Sc. in relevant fields, with GPAT/GATE/NET qualifications preferred. The fellowship offers a stipend of Rs. 37,000 plus HRA, totaling Rs. 48,100, and the appointment is for a duration of 2 years and 6 months. Applicants are required to submit a Rs. 50 application fee and bring necessary documents to the interview, with no travel allowances provided.
