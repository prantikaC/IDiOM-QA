This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University, dated February 25, 2025, announces the candidates who have been called for an interview for the position of Driver (Contractual) for the Ambulance at the Salt Lake Campus. Candidates are instructed to bring all original testimonials, and call letters are being dispatched by post. The interviews are scheduled for March 10, 2025, at 10:30 a.m. in Committee Room No. I of the University, located in Aurobindo Bhavan on the main campus. The memorandum references two advertisements for the position, dated August 6, 2024, and November 12, 2024.
