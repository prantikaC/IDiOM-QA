Jadavpur University has issued a notice regarding a Walk-in/Hybrid interview scheduled for 10th March 2025 at 2.30 PM for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) under a DVC Sponsored Research Project on Retro-fitted Electric Mobility. Interested candidates must bring their bio-data, testimonials, and a completed application form, which can be obtained for INR 50 from the university's Information Section or downloaded online with proof of payment. The application deadline for outstation candidates is 12 PM on 16th February 2025. The selected JRF will receive a fellowship of Rs. 37,000 per month for the first two years and Rs. 42,000 in the third year, with a maximum age limit of 30 years for applicants, and the position is tenable for up to three years.
