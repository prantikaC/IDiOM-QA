The Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the engagement of Guest Lecturers in the Department of History at Jadavpur University, scheduled for January 29, 2025, at 11:00 AM. Candidates interested in applying must bring their CV, original documents, and self-attested photocopies, along with valid ID proof, and should arrive by 10:30 AM. The positions require a minimum qualification of BA History Hons, MA History, and NET/SLET in History, with preference given to those holding a PhD and having research publications and teaching experience. There are 8 vacancies available, and the honorarium is set at 500/- per class for a tenure of one year. No travel allowances will be provided.
