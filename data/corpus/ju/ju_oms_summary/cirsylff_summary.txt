This circular from Jadavpur University announces a temporary part-time job opening for a Project Assistant in the JU-SYLFF Programme, requiring applicants to be students from specific faculties and at certain academic levels. The position involves providing academic and administrative support, with a pay of Rs. 500 per day for a minimum of two days a week. Interested students must submit a typed application to the Office of the Joint Registrar by 27 June 2025, with interviews scheduled for 30 June 2025. The application requires personal details, career goals, and computer skills.
