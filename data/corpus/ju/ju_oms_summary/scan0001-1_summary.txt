This Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) at Jadavpur University for a project funded by CSIR-ASPIRE, with the interview scheduled for 18th February 2025 at 3:30 p.m. The position requires a BTech in relevant fields with a minimum of 55% marks and passing of NET/GATE, and offers a fellowship of Rs. 37,000 per month for the first two years, increasing to Rs. 42,000 in the third year. Candidates must bring their bio-data, testimonials, and a completed application form, which can be obtained for INR 50. Age limits apply, with relaxations for certain categories, and no travel allowances will be provided for the interview.
