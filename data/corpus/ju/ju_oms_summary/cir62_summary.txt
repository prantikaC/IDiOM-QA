This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University, issued on February 12, 2025, informs all concerned parties that the university will be closed on February 13, 2025, as per notification REC/N/059/2025 dated February 11, 2025. Additionally, it states that the interview for the appointment of a Guest Faculty for Economics (Minor) courses under the Faculty of Science, originally scheduled for February 13, 2025, will now take place on February 18, 2025, with the time, venue, and other conditions remaining unchanged as referenced in REC/N/043/25 dated February 6, 2025.
