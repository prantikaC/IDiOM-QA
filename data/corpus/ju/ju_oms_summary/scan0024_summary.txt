This Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the position of Junior Research Fellow (JRF) in a Government of West Bengal-funded research project at Jadavpur University, supervised by Prof. (Dr.) Joydeep Chowdhury. The position is temporary, with one vacancy available, requiring candidates to hold an M.Sc in Physics with a minimum of 60% marks. The fellowship offers ₹25,000 per month for the first two years, increasing to ₹30,000 in the third year, contingent on satisfactory project progress evaluations every six months. Interested candidates must attend the interview on 15th May 2025, bringing an application form and supporting documents, which can be obtained for ₹50 or downloaded online. Candidates are advised that no travel allowances will be provided and that this position does not guarantee future employment.
