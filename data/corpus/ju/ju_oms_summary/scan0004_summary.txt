This Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the recruitment of one Project Technical Support-III position in the Department of Pharmaceutical Technology at Jadavpur University, scheduled for 18th February 2025 at 3:00 PM. The position is part of an ICMR sponsored project focused on developing non-antibiotic loaded mucoadhesive gastroretentive tablets for treating resistant Helicobacter pylori. Candidates must hold an M.Pharm. degree with GPAT qualification and be knowledgeable in matrix tablet development, with an age limit of 30 years, extendable for certain categories. The fellowship offered is Rs. 36,400 per month for a tenure of three years. Interested applicants are required to apply in person with a prescribed application form costing Rs. 50, along with original and attested copies of relevant documents, and no travel allowances will be provided.
