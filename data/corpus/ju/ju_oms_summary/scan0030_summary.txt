This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University announces a walk-in interview scheduled for June 16, 2025, for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) for a project on developing a portable electronic nose for quality assessment of spices. The position requires an M.Tech degree, preferably GATE qualified, with a background in Instrumentation, Electronics, or Electrical engineering. The fellowship offers a monthly stipend of Rs. 38,440 for the JRF and Rs. 43,400 for the subsequent Senior Research Fellow (SRF) phase. Candidates must bring their bio-data, testimonials, and a completed application form, which can be obtained for INR 50. The interview will take place at the Seminar Room-I of the Department of Instrumentation and Electronics Engineering at Jadavpur University, starting at 1:30 PM. No travel allowances will be provided for attending the interview.
