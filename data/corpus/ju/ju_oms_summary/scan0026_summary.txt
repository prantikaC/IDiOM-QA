This Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the position of Junior Research Fellow (JRF) at Jadavpur University, funded by the Department of Science & Technology and Biotechnology, Government of West Bengal, for a project on cardiovascular diseases. The interview is scheduled for 5th June 2025, and candidates must possess a Post-Graduate Degree in relevant fields, with desirable qualifications including experience in molecular biology techniques. The fellowship offers ₹25,000 per month for the first 21 months, increasing to ₹30,000 in the third year, with evaluations every six months. Candidates must bring an application form, proof of publications, and mark sheets, and the application form is available for ₹50. No travel allowances will be provided.
