This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University announces a Walk-in/Hybrid interview scheduled for 17th February 2025 at 2:30 PM for the recruitment of two Junior Research Fellow (JRF) positions under a DVC Sponsored Research Project focused on retro-fitted electric mobility. Candidates must possess specific educational qualifications and GATE qualifications, with a maximum age limit of 30 years, extendable for certain categories. The selected fellows will receive a monthly fellowship of Rs. 37,000/- for the first two years and Rs. 42,000/- for the third year, with a tenure of up to three years. Interested candidates are required to submit a completed application form along with a fee of INR 50 and necessary documents by 12 PM on 16th February 2025, and no travel allowances will be provided for the interview.
