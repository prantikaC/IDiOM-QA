This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University invites applications for the position of Junior Research Fellow (JRF) in a research project funded by CSIR, with a project title focusing on novel catalysis. The application deadline is set for January 2, 2025, and the project will last for three years under the supervision of Dr. Ananya Baksi. Candidates must possess a minimum qualification of a B.S. 4-year program or MSc in Chemistry with at least 55% marks and must have passed the NET/GATE test. The fellowship amounts to INR 37,000 for the first two years and INR 42,000 for the third year, along with additional allowances. Interested candidates are required to apply via email, including a scanned application form and transaction details for a fee of INR 50, and must bring original documents for verification during the interview.
