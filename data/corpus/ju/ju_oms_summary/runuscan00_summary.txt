Jadavpur University is inviting applications for the position of Junior Research Fellow (JRF) for a project funded by CSIR-ASPIRE, with a vacancy for one candidate under the supervision of Prof. Runu Banerjee Roy in the Department of Instrumentation and Electronics Engineering. The project, titled 'MIP based electrochemical trace detection of pesticides in harvested crops using a portable measurement interface,' has a tenure of three years and offers a fellowship of Rs. 37,000 per month for the first two years, increasing to Rs. 42,000 in the third year. Applicants must hold a BTech in relevant fields with at least 55% marks and must have passed the NET/GATE test, with a maximum age limit of 28 years, relaxable for certain categories. The last date for application submission is April 21, 2025, and candidates are required to apply via email with necessary documents, including a prescribed application form available for INR 50.
