This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University announces the invitation for online applications for two student intern positions under the Seafire Power Sponsored Research Project focused on water desalination systems. The interns will work under the supervision of Dr. Nirmalendu Biswas and Dr. Animesh Roy in the Department of Power Engineering. Candidates must have a bachelor's degree in relevant engineering fields, with preference given to those with experience in experimental work or computational fluid dynamics. The fellowship is set at Rs. 10,000 per month for a duration of three months, and applications must be submitted via email by 5 PM on May 12, 2025.
