Jadavpur University is inviting applications for the position of Junior Research Fellow (JRF) for a project funded by CSIR-ASPIRE, with a vacancy for one candidate under the supervision of Prof. Runu Banerjee Roy in the Department of Instrumentation and Electronics Engineering. The project, titled 'MIP based electrochemical trace detection of pesticides in harvested crops using a portable measurement interface,' has a tenure of three years and offers a fellowship of Rs. 37,000 per month for the first two years, increasing to Rs. 42,000 in the third year. Applicants must have a BTech in relevant fields or an M.Sc in Chemistry with at least 55% marks and must have passed the NET/GATE test. The application deadline is May 10, 2025, and interested candidates should apply via email with the required documents, including a hardcopy application form available for INR 50.
