This Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) for a project on AI techniques related to air pollutants and climatic factors, funded by CSIR-ASPIRE. The JRF position requires candidates to have a relevant degree with at least 55% marks and to have qualified UGC/CSIR NET and/or GATE. The appointment is for a period of three years, initially for one year, and is subject to satisfactory performance and funding availability. The age limit is 28 years, with relaxations for certain categories. The fellowship amount is Rs. 31,000 per month. The interview is scheduled for February 5, 2025, at 11 AM at Jadavpur University, and candidates must bring their bio-data, testimonials, and a completed application form, which can be obtained for a fee of Rs. 50. No travel allowances will be provided.
