Jadavpur University is inviting applications for the position of one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) for a project on stochastic resetting, sponsored by DST-SERB, under the supervision of Dr. Shrabani Mondal. Candidates must have an M.Sc. in Chemistry or Physics with at least 55% marks and must be NET/GATE qualified, with desirable experience in non-equilibrium statistical mechanics and programming. The age limit is 28 years, with relaxations for certain categories. The fellowship is Rs. 37,000 plus 24% HRA, and the tenure lasts until the project's termination. Applications must be submitted by email by March 20, 2025, along with a fee of Rs. 50, and shortlisted candidates will be informed about the interview date via email.
