This Office Memorandum from the District Welfare Officer of Backward Classes Welfare, Kolkata, dated June 10, 2025, informs all universities and colleges under the Kolkata Municipal Corporation about the commencement of the application process for the Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC students for the academic year 2025-26. Institutions are requested to encourage eligible students to apply online, with the requirement that college authorities verify the applications on the portal and submit hardcopies to the office. Additionally, it highlights the new mandate for biometric authentication for all OASIS scholarship applications, emphasizing the importance of ensuring that no eligible student is overlooked in the application process.
