This Office Memorandum informs all concerned parties about a health camp organized for Jadavpur University employees at the Salt Lake Campus, based on the approval of the Pro Vice Chancellor and the Chairman of the Employees’ Welfare Committee. The event, scheduled for 12th June 2025, will feature an awareness session on cancer led by oncologist Dr. Abhra Mondal, along with a general health check-up that includes various medical assessments. The program aims to promote health awareness and encourage participation from the JU community.
