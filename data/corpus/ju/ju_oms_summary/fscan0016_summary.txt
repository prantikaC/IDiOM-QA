This Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the recruitment of one project fellow at Jadavpur University, scheduled for 19th March 2025 at 1 pm in the Department of Physics. The position is part of a project on electrocatalysts for hydrogen production, funded by Magellanium Technologies Pvt. Ltd. Candidates must hold an M.Sc. in Physics or an M.Tech in Nanoscience and Technology, with a preference for those qualified in NET or GATE and possessing relevant research experience. The fellowship offers a consolidated monthly stipend of Rs. 42,550 for an initial tenure of one year, with possible extensions based on performance. There are specific age limits and relaxation provisions for certain categories as per government rules. Interested candidates must obtain a prescribed application form for Rs. 50 and present required documents during the interview.
