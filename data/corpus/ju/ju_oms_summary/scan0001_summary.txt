This Office Memorandum announces a walk-in interview for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow in the Department of Life Science and Biotechnology at Jadavpur University, scheduled for 25th February 2025 at 3:00 PM. The position is part of a DBT sponsored research project focused on the post-transcriptional regulation of SKS1 mRNA in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, supervised by Prof. Biswadip Das. Candidates must hold an M.Sc. in any branch of Life Science with relevant experience, and the age limit is 30 years, with relaxations for certain categories. The fellowship is set at Rs.37,000 plus 27% HRA, and the tenure is three years. Interested applicants are required to bring a completed application form, available for Rs.50, along with original and attested documents to the interview, noting that no travel allowances will be provided.
