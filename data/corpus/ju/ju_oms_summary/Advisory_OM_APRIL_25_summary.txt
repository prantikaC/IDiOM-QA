This Office Memorandum addresses several compliance issues related to the Fellowships and Associateships of Research Fellows hosted at various Universities, Institutes, and Colleges. It highlights that many Research Fellows are failing to submit necessary documents for their upgradation or continuation within the stipulated timelines, and are transferring their fellowships without obtaining prior approval from CSIR, which violates established guidelines. Additionally, it points out that Fellows are bypassing the required communication channels by contacting multiple authorities directly, and there are inaccuracies in the information submitted on the New FMS portal. The memorandum emphasizes the need for adherence to these guidelines to avoid adverse consequences, including delays in processing fellowship claims or termination of fellowships.
