This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University, dated May 13, 2025, announces the opening of online applications for one post of Project Assistant (PA) for a research project titled 'Cooling Tower Fog Harvesting using Wettability-Engineered Meshes', funded by the Damodar Valley Corporation. The selected candidate will work under Prof. Ranjan Ganguly and must possess a BE/B. Tech in relevant engineering fields with a minimum of 60% marks. The position offers a consolidated fellowship of Rs. 20,000 per month and has an age limit of 28 years, with relaxations for certain categories. The tenure is provisionally until February 10, 2026, subject to project funding. Interested candidates must submit their applications, including a prescribed form and supporting documents, by June 10, 2025, with a nominal application fee of Rs. 50.
