This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University announces a walk-in interview scheduled for June 17, 2025, at 4:00 PM in the Department of Mechanical Engineering for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) under the RESPOND project focused on developing a combustion instability model for a LOX-Methane engine. Candidates must possess a BE/B Tech or ME/M Tech in relevant engineering fields and should have qualified in GATE. The selected JRF will receive a fellowship of Rs 37,000 plus 27% HRA per month, with an initial duration of two years, extendable based on project progress. Interested candidates are required to bring their bio-data, testimonials, and a completed application form, which can be obtained for Rs. 50, and must note that no travel allowances will be provided for attending the interview.
