This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University announces a walk-in interview scheduled for March 6, 2025, at 1:00 PM for the recruitment of three Field Investigators and one Research Assistant for a research project funded by ICSSR. The project focuses on the impact of the Jal Jeevan Mission on the living conditions of tribal women in West Bengal's semi-arid region. The eligibility criteria include a postgraduate degree in Social Science for Field Investigators and a PhD/MPhil/NET along with a postgraduate degree for the Research Assistant. The positions are temporary, with a fellowship of Rs 20,000 per month for Field Investigators and Rs 37,000 per month for the Research Assistant. Candidates must bring all necessary documents and a research application form available for Rs 50 at the university's information counter.
