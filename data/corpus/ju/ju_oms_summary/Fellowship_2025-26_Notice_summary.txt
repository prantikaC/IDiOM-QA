The Office Memorandum invites applications for the SYLFF-JU Fellowship for the academic year 2025-26, aimed at supporting young researchers in social sciences and humanities with leadership potential. The fellowship offers a one-year award of USD 1800 for MA students and a three-year PhD fellowship totaling USD 22800, contingent upon meeting specific eligibility criteria, including enrollment and completion of necessary admission tests. An orientation workshop for proposal writing is scheduled for 7 March 2025, and application forms will be available online from 10 February 2025, with a submission deadline of 21 March 2025. A fee of Rs 200 is required for application processing.
