This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University invites online applications for a Student Internship lasting up to 2 months in a DST-SERB funded project focused on IoT for smart agriculture, supervised by Dr. Joydeep Basu. Eligible candidates must be third or fourth-year undergraduate engineering students specializing in relevant fields, with desirable experience in IoT design or Machine Learning. The internship offers an assistantship of up to Rs. 5,000 per month, with applications due by December 20, 2024. Selected candidates will need to provide a no-objection certificate from their institution and verification documents. No travel or daily allowances are provided beyond the assistantship.
