This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University, dated February 12, 2025, invites applications for the AICTE Doctoral Fellowship for the academic year 2023-24. Candidates who registered for the Ph.D. Program during this period under the specified thrust area are eligible to apply via a Google form available on the university's website. The fellowship is expected to commence in the academic year 2024-25, with no retrospective benefits as per AICTE directives. Interested candidates must submit their Google Forms promptly and provide hard copies of required documents to the Research Section for the creation of a new student ID.
