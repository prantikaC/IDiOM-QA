This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University announces a walk-in/hybrid interview scheduled for June 10, 2025, at 2:30 PM for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow under a WBDST Project focused on enhanced phase change materials for solar thermal energy storage. The selected candidate will work under Dr. Nirnmalendu Biswas and must possess a Bachelor's degree in relevant engineering fields along with a Master's degree and GATE qualification. The fellowship is set at Rs. 25,000 per month, with an age limit of 30 years, extendable for certain categories. Interested candidates are required to submit a hardcopy application form, available for INR 50, along with necessary documents, and must attend the interview in person at the specified venue. Outstation candidates can apply by submitting their documents by April 20, 2025.
