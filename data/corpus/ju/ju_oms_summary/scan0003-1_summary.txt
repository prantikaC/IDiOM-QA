This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University announces a walk-in interview scheduled for 05th March 2025 at 02:00 p.m. for the recruitment of two Junior Research Fellows (JRF) under a DRDO funded research project titled 'Claim Detection and Verification using Deep NLP: an Indian Perspective.' The memorandum outlines the essential qualifications required for applicants, including an M.E/M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering, and mentions a desirable GATE/NET/SET score along with experience in Natural Language Processing. The age limit for candidates is set at 30 years, with relaxations for certain categories. The fellowship amount is Rs. 31,000.00, with a potential 10% HRA, and the tenure extends until January 2026. Interested candidates must submit their applications via email by 03rd March 2025, and are required to bring original documents for verification at the interview.
