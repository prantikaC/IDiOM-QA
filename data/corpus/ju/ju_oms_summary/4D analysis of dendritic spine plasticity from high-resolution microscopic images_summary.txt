This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University announces a walk-in interview scheduled for January 27, 2025, for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) for a project on dendritic spine plasticity, sponsored by SERB, Govt. of India. The position requires candidates to have qualifications in B.E./B.Tech/MCA/MSc.(CS)/M.E./M.Tech. with GATE/NET qualification, and desirable skills in Digital Image Processing and Machine Learning. The fellowship offers a monthly stipend of Rs. 38,440 for a duration of 1 year and 8 months. Candidates must bring application forms, available for Rs. 50, along with necessary documents to the interview at the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, with no travel allowances provided.
