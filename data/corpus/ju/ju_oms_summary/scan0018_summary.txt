This Office Memorandum from Jadavpur University announces a Walk-in/Hybrid interview scheduled for 21st April 2025 at 2:30 PM for the recruitment of one Junior Research Fellow under the WBDST Project focused on enhanced phase change materials for solar thermal energy storage. The position requires a Bachelor's degree in relevant engineering fields and GATE qualification, with a monthly fellowship of Rs. 25,000. The age limit is set at 30 years, with relaxations for certain categories. Interested candidates must bring their bio-data, testimonials, and a completed application form, which can be obtained for INR 50. Applications from outstation candidates are also accepted until 12 PM on 20th April 2025.
